**DAY 90 – THE INVITATION**

**Scripture**

**Romans 10:9-13 CSB**

If you confess with your mouth, "Jesus is Lord," and believe in your heart that God raised him from the dead, you will be saved. [10] One believes with the heart, resulting in righteousness, and one confesses with the mouth, resulting in salvation. [11] For the Scripture says, Everyone who believes on him will not be put to shame, [12] since there is no distinction between Jew and Greek, because the same Lord of all richly blesses all who call on him. [13] For everyone who calls on the name of the Lord will be saved.

**Come to Jesus**

As we come to the final day of our journey together, I find myself thinking about the many wonderful truths we have discovered in God's Word. We have walked alongside men and women of faith, witnessed their victories and failures, and considered the extraordinary love and patience of our Heavenly Father. We have seen how easily human pride can lead us astray, how fear and doubt can weaken our faith, and how God's mercy continues to reach toward those who seek Him.

Through it all, one beautiful truth has remained unchanged. God loves us, and He has made a way for us to come home to Him through His Son, Jesus Christ.

I sometimes wonder how many people spend their entire lives searching for something they cannot quite identify. They work hard, raise families, gather possessions, and pursue the things they believe will bring happiness. Some become wealthy and successful, while others struggle simply to make it through another day. Yet whether we have much or little, there can remain an emptiness within us that nothing in this world seems able to fill.

We may try to satisfy that longing through entertainment, recognition, relationships, or the endless pursuit of something better. But the excitement eventually fades. The possessions grow old, the applause becomes quiet, and the pleasures we once chased lose their attraction. We discover that even the finest things this world offers cannot give us eternal life or restore our broken relationship with God.

There is a reason for that emptiness. We were created to know our Heavenly Father, to walk in fellowship with Him, and to enjoy His love. Yet sin has separated us from the One who gave us life. We have all gone our own way at times, choosing our desires over His will. Some of us have wandered much farther than others, but none of us can stand before a holy God claiming to have lived without sin.

And this is where the wonderful message of Jesus Christ begins.

In John 3:16, Jesus reveals the extraordinary depth of God's love for the world. Our Heavenly Father loved us so much that He gave His one and only Son so that everyone who believes in Him might have eternal life rather than perish.

Think about what that means. God did not wait for us to become good enough to deserve His love. He did not demand that we first repair every broken part of our lives or somehow erase the wrongs of our past. He knew our condition, understood our failures, and still loved us enough to send His Son.

Jesus came into this world and lived the perfect life we could never live. He showed compassion to the hurting, welcomed those whom others rejected, healed the sick, and revealed the loving character of His Father. Then He willingly suffered and died upon a cross, bearing the penalty for our sins. On the third day, He rose from the grave, demonstrating His victory over sin and death.

What a wonderful gift our Heavenly Father has offered us!

And yet, like any gift, it must be received.

Perhaps you have been reading these devotionals for some time. You may enjoy the stories of Scripture and believe that God exists. You might even attend church or have grown up in a Christian home. But there is an important question that each of us must answer for ourselves.

Have you personally placed your faith in Jesus Christ as your Lord and Savior?

Knowing about Jesus is not the same as knowing Him. We can understand the stories of His miracles, admire His teaching, and appreciate the kindness He showed others without ever surrendering our hearts to Him.

The Apostle Paul explains in Romans chapter 10 that salvation comes through faith in Jesus Christ. We are called to believe in our hearts that God raised Him from the dead and to confess Him as Lord. Everyone who calls upon the name of the Lord will be saved.

There is no special social standing required, no educational achievement to obtain, and no amount of money that can purchase this wonderful gift. The invitation reaches across every nation, every generation, and every walk of life. Rich or poor, young or old, respected or forgotten by the world, we all come to God in the same way—through faith in His Son.

Perhaps you are carrying a past filled with mistakes. There may be things you have done that bring tears to your eyes whenever you remember them. Perhaps you have hurt people you loved, wasted opportunities, or wandered so far from God that you wonder whether He would ever welcome you.

My friend, do not allow your past to keep you from coming to Jesus.

Remember the father in the parable of the lost son. When his wayward son finally turned toward home, the father did not stand at the door with folded arms, waiting to humiliate him. He ran to meet him, embraced him, and welcomed him home.

What a beautiful picture of our Heavenly Father's love for those who turn to Him in repentance and faith.

You do not have to wait until tomorrow. You do not have to become a better person before asking Jesus to forgive you. Come to Him with your sins, your doubts, your fears, and your brokenness. Believe that He died for your sins and rose again. Turn away from the life that has separated you from God, and place your trust in Jesus Christ as your Lord and Savior.

And if you have already received this wonderful gift, may I encourage you to remember the love you first had for Him? We can become so busy with the cares of life that our time with God grows shorter and our hearts become distracted. Let us return each day to His Word, spend time in prayer, and allow the Holy Spirit to continue His work within us.

Throughout these ninety days, we have considered what it means to walk in the light of God's truth. We have learned that our good works cannot purchase salvation, but that a living faith should produce love, compassion, forgiveness, and a desire to serve others. We have seen that our Heavenly Father is gracious and patient, yet also holy and just. Above all, we have been reminded that Jesus Christ is the precious gift through whom we receive forgiveness and eternal life.

And now, as we close this book, I want to leave you with one final thought.

Our journey through God's Word does not end with the final page of a devotional. Each morning brings another opportunity to draw closer to our Heavenly Father, to seek His guidance, and to reflect the love of His Son in the way we live.

One day, the pages of our earthly lives will also come to an end. The things we have accumulated will remain behind, and the accomplishments we once considered so important will fade into history. But for those who belong to Jesus Christ, something far more wonderful awaits.

We will be welcomed into the presence of our Heavenly Father, where there will be no more sorrow, no more pain, and no more death. We will finally be home.

If you have never placed your faith in Jesus Christ, I invite you to come to Him today. There is no greater love you will ever know, no greater forgiveness you will ever receive, and no greater treasure you will ever find.

May the Lord bless you, keep you in His loving care, and guide your steps as you continue walking in His light.

**Reflection Questions**

1. John 3:16 reminds us that God loved the world enough to give His one and only Son. When you consider the sacrifice Jesus made for you, what does that reveal about the depth of your Heavenly Father's love?

2. Have you personally placed your faith in Jesus Christ as your Lord and Savior, trusting in His death and resurrection for the forgiveness of your sins? If not, what is keeping you from responding to His invitation today?

3. As you look back over your own journey of faith, how has God's love changed your life? What can you do in the days ahead to grow closer to Him and help others discover the wonderful gift of salvation through Jesus Christ?

**Prayer**

Heavenly Father,

As we come to the end of this journey through Your Word, our hearts are filled with gratitude for Your wonderful love, mercy, and grace. Thank You for sending Your Son, Jesus Christ, to die for our sins and for raising Him from the grave so that we might have the promise of eternal life. Forgive us for the times we have wandered from You, placed our own desires above Your will, or failed to reflect Your love to those around us. For those who have not yet come to know Jesus as their Savior, I pray that You will open their hearts to the truth of the gospel. May they turn from their sins, believe in His death and resurrection, and place their faith in Him as Lord and Savior. For those of us who already belong to Christ, renew our love for You and strengthen our desire to walk faithfully in Your ways. Help us to be a light in this world, sharing Your kindness, compassion, and the wonderful hope we have received. And when our journey through this life is complete, may we rejoice together in Your eternal presence, praising the name of Jesus forever. In His precious and holy name we pray.

Amen.
