**DAY 53 – FEAR OF THE LORD**

**Scripture**

**1 John 5:1-4 CSB**

Everyone who believes that Jesus is the Christ has been born of God, and everyone who loves the Father also loves the one born of him. [2] This is how we know that we love God's children: when we love God and obey his commands. [3] For this is what love for God is: to keep his commands. And his commands are not a burden, [4] because everyone who has been born of God conquers the world. This is the victory that has conquered the world: our faith.

**Deuteronomy 10:12 CSB**

"And now, Israel, what does the LORD your God ask of you except to fear the LORD your God by walking in all his ways, to love him, and to worship the LORD your God with all your heart and all your soul?”

**Victory in Faith**

Who is God that we should bow before Him? When asked in doubt or rebellion, this question can lead us away from God. Yet it is also a question worth answering. Revelation 4 gives us a powerful reason:

"Our Lord and God, you are worthy to receive glory and honor and power, because you have created all things, and by your will they exist and were created."

God is the Creator of all things. In Him is life. Nothing would exist without Him. Our very days are a gift from God, and our continued existence depends upon the One who created us.

When people hear the phrase fear of the Lord, they may picture a harsh taskmaster like the rulers of this world who have controlled people through cruelty and terror. God is to be feared, and Scripture warns us that His judgment is real. Yet God also desires people to be saved, and Jesus said that He came so that we might have life and have it in abundance. Jesus described Himself as gentle and humble in heart and called us to learn from Him.

To understand what Moses means by fear in our reading today, we need to look at the way the word is being used. The Hebrew word can mean fear, but it can also carry the sense of reverence, awe, respect, and recognizing God's authority. Moses helps us understand his meaning by putting the fear of the Lord alongside walking in God's ways, loving Him, and worshiping Him with all our heart and soul. Here, fearing God is not standing in abject horror, waiting for something terrible to happen. It is recognizing who God is and responding to Him with reverence, love, worship, and obedience.

God is a great King, above every earthly ruler, and His words are to be taken seriously. Yet His commands are not the demands of a cruel ruler. So what is God's great command for us? John had already summarized it: to believe in the name of His Son, Jesus Christ, and to love one another. Those who believe that Jesus is the Christ have been born of God. As His children, we love our Father and seek to obey Him, and that love is also shown in the way we love His other children.

We do not approach God as people who must live in terror of Him. Through Christ, we have been brought into God's family as His children. We belong to His Kingdom and have received His Spirit. We obey not because we are desperately trying to earn His love or our salvation, but because we know the Father who loves us. His commands are not a burden.

And we are not only children of God in this world; we are also ambassadors for Christ. As we obey God and follow His ways, our lives should give others a glimpse of Christ in us. Jesus is the way to life, and we are called to be lights in this world, pointing others toward Him. We love God and seek to do His will because He first loved us.

**Reflection Questions**

1. How do I view God? How would I characterize Him?

2. Are there verses in the Bible that I could use to help others understand the true character of God and what it means to fear Him?

3. What should I be doing in my life to please God and honor Him as the great King of Heaven and Earth?

**Prayer**
