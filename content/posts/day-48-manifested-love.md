**DAY 48 – MANIFESTED LOVE**

**Scripture**

**1 John 3:11-18 CSB**

For this is the message you have heard from the beginning: We should love one another, [12] unlike Cain, who was of the evil one and murdered his brother. And why did he murder him? Because his deeds were evil, and his brother's were righteous. [13] Do not be surprised, brothers and sisters, if the world hates you. [14] We know that we have passed from death to life because we love our brothers and sisters. The one who does not love remains in death. [15] Everyone who hates his brother or sister is a murderer, and you know that no murderer has eternal life residing in him. [16] This is how we have come to know love: He laid down his life for us. We should also lay down our lives for our brothers and sisters. [17] If anyone has this world's goods and sees a fellow believer in need but withholds compassion from him-how does God's love reside in him? [18] Little children, let us not love in word or speech, but in action and in truth.

**Message of Love**

John told us earlier in his letter that we know we are in Christ if we keep His commands. As we keep His word, God’s love is made complete in us. He continues by saying that those who claim to belong to Jesus should walk as Jesus did. John also understands that we are fragile, and when we do sin, we have Jesus, who speaks to the Father on our behalf and is the atoning sacrifice for our sins. It is a somber thought to realize that my sins were paid for through the suffering of the Lord. It causes me to pause and give deep thanks for the love He has shown me.

John continues by reminding us that we should love one another, with a special love for our brothers and sisters in Christ. We are called to share with those in need, seek opportunities to help, and show hospitality to our fellow believers (Romans 12:13).

John gives us Cain as an example of what happens when hatred takes hold of the heart. Cain murdered his brother because his own deeds were evil and his brother’s were righteous. We are called to live in the opposite way. We should love our Christian family so much that we would be willing to lay down our lives for one another. We should not withhold compassion when a fellow believer is alone, without food or clothing, or left to face the harshness of the world without help.

We should not be surprised when the world rejects our faith. Jesus Himself was rejected, even though He laid down His life for the world, healed the sick, and spoke words of love, grace, and peace. It is a puzzling thing that such love can be rejected. As followers of the Lord, we too will at times face rejection because of our faith. We are not greater than the Lord. If He was rejected, we should not be surprised when we are rejected as well.

We are called to let the faith in our hearts show itself through actions that come from love and truth. Good intentions alone will not feed someone who is hungry or bring comfort to someone who is suffering. John reminds us that our love should be shown not just in words, but in what we do.

And this love does not stop with fellow believers. Our Father in Heaven loved us before we loved Him. He called us to become part of His family through His Son, Jesus, and the work of the Holy Spirit. We are called to do good to all people, even those who oppose or mistreat us. Our hope is that they might see Jesus in the way we treat them and come to know the love He has shown us.

**Reflection Questions**

1. Is my love for others only in my thoughts and words, or do I turn that love into action?

2. How can I use my time or finances to help fellow believers in need, both near me and in other parts of the world?

3. Does my love extend beyond my brothers and sisters in Christ to those who have not yet come to know Him?

**Prayer**

Heavenly Father,

I know that I often have ideas of doing great things for You and for others, but these thoughts are pushed to the back of my mind when the things of this world demand my attention. Help me to have genuine love in my heart for every person, but especially for my brothers and sisters in the Lord. Give me the love and compassion to turn my good intentions into actions that help those in need. Let me be a loving member of the family of God, showing others the love that Jesus has shown me. In Jesus’ name I pray.

Amen.
