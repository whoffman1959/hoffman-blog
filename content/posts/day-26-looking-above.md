**DAY 26 – LOOKING ABOVE**

**Scripture**

**1 Peter 4:7–11 CSB**

The end of all things is near; therefore, be alert and sober-minded for prayer. [8] Above all, maintain constant love for one another, since love covers a multitude of sins. [9] Be hospitable to one another without complaining. [10] Just as each one has received a gift, use it to serve others, as good stewards of the varied grace of God. [11] If anyone speaks, let it be as one who speaks God's words; if anyone serves, let it be from the strength God provides, so that God may be glorified through Jesus Christ in everything. To him be the glory and the power forever and ever. Amen.

**John 19:30 CSB**

When Jesus had received the sour wine, he said, "It is finished." Then bowing his head, he gave up his spirit.

**The End is Near**

The end of all things is at hand, but what does this phrase truly mean? It could point to the destruction of Jerusalem in AD 70 by the Roman army, or the final renewal of the heavens and earth when they are rolled up like a garment. It may even speak of the glorious return of Christ when all people will be judged. While the Bible holds many mysteries and scholarly debates, one profound truth remains certain: when our time comes to leave this world, we know personally that the end of all things is at hand.

Imagine the cancer patient who has received the bad news that the treatments are not working, or perhaps it is something else, but we know that we will soon put off this earthly body. Think of the older person who has seen those they once knew pass from this life. The world has become a stranger, different from the world of their youth. When these things happen, a person knows that the end is near.

Those who miraculously survive for a few more years do not see things as they once did. Some realize that their only hope is in God. They become alert to the presence of God, who can save them in this life and the next. They become humble and attentive to God's voice in prayer. Suddenly, hope and joy return. They believe that Jesus died for their sins, and that there will be life beyond this short span of time on Earth.

When we realize that God has forgiven us of our dark past through His Son, it is easy to love others and overlook their faults. Our sins are covered by what Jesus did on the cross—only by what Jesus did on the cross. When Jesus said, “It is finished,” the payment for our wrongs was made in full. Jesus stayed true to the end for us out of love. He loves us so much that He took our place and died for us so that we can walk the streets of Heaven.

We are to show this same love for one another, ready to forgive repeatedly and to give without complaint. We use the gifts that God has given us to help others. We overlook the wrongs of others in forgiveness.

When we are in the Lord, our tongues are controlled, not allowing blessings and curses to flow at the same time. Our words point to our Father in Heaven for the benefit of others. We realize that we are judged by others even in our normal conversations. Our very lives become a statement for God.

As we serve God, it is through humble recognition that it is God who gives us the strength and means to do so. A servant of God realizes that it is not by their own power that they serve the Lord. It is a gift from God. The Holy Spirit has appointed gifts to Christians to lift up others. It is not by our own power.

A true measure of our love and understanding of the Lord is whether we are on the side of the scale that lifts up Jesus to others and not ourselves. Glory belongs to Jesus. It is Jesus who has done the work. It is God the Father who saves us through His Son and builds us up through the Spirit. Have I showed God’s love today?

**Reflection Questions**

1. What gifts has God given me to show love to others? Maybe it is being a good friend, giving my time or finances to someone in need, teaching the Word, or praying for others. Maybe it is something else, such as offering a word of encouragement. What gifts has God given me, and how can I use them to lift up others?
2. Do I really love others deeply in my heart, or do I simply want to love others more deeply. Do I need to seek God's help in this area of my life?
3. Do I know today that, if my time on Earth is at hand, I will be with Jesus? Are my sins are covered because of what the Lord did for me on the cross?

**Prayer**

Heavenly Father,

Thank You for reminding us that our time on this Earth is very short compared to eternity. Let us be humble and grateful for the gifts You have given us to serve others. Let us give generously from what You have entrusted to us. Let the hardness of our hearts be removed so that we truly love others, not just in word, but through our actions. Fill us with Your Holy Spirit and help us recognize the gifts You have given us through the Spirit so that we may use them to help and lift up others. Let us always look to You for our hope. In Jesus' name we pray.

Amen.
