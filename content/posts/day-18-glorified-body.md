**DAY 18 – GLORIFIED BODY**

**Scripture**

**2 Corinthians 5:1 (CSB)**

For we know that if our earthly tent we live in is destroyed, we have a building from God, an eternal dwelling in the heavens, not made with hands.

**1 John 3:2 (CSB)**

Dear friends, we are God's children now, and what we will be has not yet been revealed. We know that when he appears, we will be like him because we will see him as he is.

**New Beginning**

When I walk out to start the car, it sputters and rattles. It’s no longer shiny or new, but it still carries me where I need to go — most of the time. My body feels much the same. After years and surgeries, it doesn’t move like it once did. It stiffens, tires quickly, and reminds me that time has its say.

Many who’ve reached the later seasons of life understand this deeply. We live in the twilight between memory and eternity. Sometimes, in the quiet moments, our thoughts drift back to younger days — the joys that still bring a smile, the regrets we wish we could rewrite, and the whispered “if only” prayers that linger at the edges of the heart.

But then a softer truth rises — a holy reminder we desperately need: this is not the end.

There is a new beginning coming. When the final breath leaves the body, those who belong to Christ will awaken renewed — made like Him, whole and holy. The aches will be gone, the scars covered in glory, and the weight of past failures lifted forever. Every imperfection will be replaced with the radiance of the Father’s presence.

We will see Him as He is — and we will finally be as we were meant to be.
The faith we carry now — fragile at times, unseen, like a small seed in tired soil — will one day burst into eternal life. What feels small and hidden today will bloom into the beauty of forever. Nothing done in faith is wasted. Nothing trusted to the Father goes unseen.

1 John reminds us that even now we are the children of God. The Kingdom of Heaven is not only something we will enter someday — it has already begun in us. It is the Spirit dwelling in us, reshaping us. It is Christ living within the believer. Jesus said in Luke 17:20–21, “The kingdom of God is not coming with something observable. No one will say, ‘See here!’ or ‘There!’ For you see, the kingdom of God is in your midst.”

It’s like a light switching on — the moment we realize we are already accepted, already brought into the Father’s kingdom. Even now, we are strengthened by the Holy Spirit to live in a way that pleases Him. Even now, His life pulses within ours, drawing us forward.

The question is this: Are we spending our days only looking for a future kingdom… or are we living in it right now? Are we aware that every step, every breath, every quiet act of faith is part of walking with Jesus even before we see Him face to face?

One day we will stand before Him, break bread with Him, walk in His light without the weight of this earthly body. How glorious that day will be.
But until then — the kingdom has already begun.

**Reflection Questions**

1. How does knowing I will one day be made whole in Christ bring peace to my present struggles?

2. What memories or regrets can I surrender to God, trusting that He will redeem them in eternity?

3. How can I nurture the 'seed' of faith today so that it continues to grow toward eternal life?

**Prayer**

Heavenly Father,

Thank You for the promise of new life beyond this one. When my body aches and my strength fades, remind me that I am more than this earthly tent. Help me to live with hope, trusting that You are preparing something far greater — a body renewed, a heart made whole, and a joy that will never fade. May I live each day in gratitude, knowing that even in weakness, Your glory is growing within me.

Amen.
