**DAY 7 – THE CREATOR WHO CARES**

**Scripture**

**Psalm 104:24 (CSB)**

"How countless are your works, LORD! In wisdom you have made them all; the earth is full of your creatures."

**Psalm 121:1–2 (CSB)**

"I lift my eyes toward the mountains. Where will my help come from? [2] My help comes from the LORD, the Maker of heaven and earth."

**God is Love**

Whoever has had a pet from its earliest days to its final moments has witnessed a small picture of a full life. I remember my first real pet—her name was Gidget. She was full of energy and joy. As kids, my brother and I would crawl around on all fours, and she would chase us, tugging on our pant legs with all the enthusiasm her little heart could hold. She slept in our beds, comforted us when we were sad, and became part of the family.

One day, she was hit by a car. Her hip was badly injured, and the veterinarian told us she wouldn’t survive the night. But she did. She recovered, lived many more years, and remained a source of joy until old age finally caught up with her. Looking back, I often think of how deeply we loved her—and how, in a much greater and more perfect way, the Father loves us. He cares for us, watches over us, and desires good for us, even when life brings pain.

Divorce, the loss of work, wars, famine—the list of human suffering is long. At times, life can feel like one hardship after another, a trail of tears that seems to have no end. Yet scattered among the sorrow are moments of laughter, peace, and joy. Scripture reminds us that there is “a time to weep and a time to laugh,” and through every season—whether brokenness or gladness—the Father remains close.

The Lord who shaped the stars and breathed life into the smallest of creatures watches over us with the same careful love. The One who formed the earth in His wisdom also holds our lives in His hands. In hardship, He is our Helper; in joy, He is our Sustainer. Not a moment passes that is outside His awareness or beyond His care.

We cannot repay God for His goodness—He owns all things. There is nothing we can give that He needs. Yet there is one offering He delights in: our love. When we return to Him with gratitude, when we trust Him through both the valleys and the mountaintops, when we extend compassion to others as He has extended it to us, we give Him the only gift that truly pleases His heart.

A life lived in gratitude, trust, and love is the offering He welcomes. And in return, He gives us Himself—our Shepherd, our Sustainer, our Father through every season of life.

**Reflection Questions**

1. How does knowing that God created everything in wisdom help me trust Him during difficult times?

2. When life feels uncertain, do I turn my eyes to the Creator for help, or do I rely on my own strength?

3. In what simple ways can I love God back today—through worship, kindness, or caring for His creation?

**Prayer**

Creator God,

You made the heavens, the earth, and everything in them. Your wisdom is beyond measure, and yet You know me by name. In times of sorrow, lift my eyes to You, the Maker of heaven and earth. In times of joy, remind me to give thanks. Help me to love You with all my heart and to reflect Your care in how I treat others and Your creation. Thank You for being both the Creator of all things and the Helper of my soul.

Amen.
