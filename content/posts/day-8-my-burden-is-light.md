**DAY 8 – MY BURDEN IS LIGHT**

**Scripture**

**Matthew 11:28–30 (CSB)**

"Come to me, all of you who are weary and burdened, and I will give you rest. [29] Take up my yoke and learn from me, because I am lowly and humble in heart, and you will find rest for your souls. [30] For my yoke is easy and my burden is light."

**Heavy Laden**

During my early walk in the faith—and even now—I often had to fight the urge to earn my salvation instead of resting in what the Father has already done. There were seasons when I tried so hard to prove myself worthy, as though God’s love depended on my efforts. But salvation has never been about my goodness. It has always been about the Father’s grace.

He sent His Son, who willingly died for me. And He called me back to Himself through a Christian sailor I once shared a room with during my Navy years. That friend invited me to church, opened the Bible with me, and even took me to a Christian bookstore to buy a Bible that I hadn’t read in a long time. Through that simple kindness, the Father lifted me when I slipped, restored me when I fell, and He holds me even today—imperfections, mistakes, and all.

During Jesus’ ministry, the Jewish people were weary and weighed down—not only by life’s struggles, but by the heavy religious expectations placed upon them by their leaders. The Pharisees had stacked rule upon rule, binding the people to man-made burdens instead of freeing them to know the heart of God. These leaders clung to their power and position so tightly that they rejected—and ultimately crucified—the very Messiah sent to save them.

In the Sermon on the Mount, we see how impossible it is for fallen humanity to meet God’s perfect standard. None of us can do it. Yet Jesus came to do what we could not. He paid the price for our failures and replaced the unbearable weight of law with the freedom of grace.

Our Lord summed up all those burdensome commands into two simple truths:
To love God with all our heart, soul, and mind, and to love others as ourselves.

Through His humility and love, He invites us to lay down our heavy loads and take up His gentle yoke. In Him, we find rest—not because life becomes easy, but because His presence makes the burden light.

We are saved not by our accomplishments, our consistency, or our best efforts,
but because the Father, in His mercy, makes salvation possible—grace upon grace, even when we try to earn what is freely given.

**Reflection Questions**

1. What burdens or expectations am I carrying that Jesus never asked me to bear?

2. How can I rest in Christ’s grace instead of striving to earn God’s approval?

3. What does it look like for me to “take up His yoke” in daily life with humility and love?

**Prayer**

Lord Jesus,

Thank You for inviting me to lay my burdens at Your feet. Forgive me for the times I’ve tried to earn what You freely offer—Your grace and rest. Teach me to walk beside You with a humble heart, learning from Your gentleness. Help me to release the weight of guilt, fear, and perfectionism, and to find peace in Your presence. May Your love guide me as I love God the Father and others, just as You taught.

Amen.
