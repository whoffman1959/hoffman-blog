**DAY 32 – THE TRUTH**

**Scripture**

**2 Peter 1:12-15 CSB**

Therefore I will always remind you about these things, even though you know them and are established in the truth you now have. [13] I think it is right, as long as I am in this bodily tent, to wake you up with a reminder, [14] since I know that I will soon lay aside my tent, as our Lord Jesus Christ has indeed made clear to me. [15] And I will also make every effort so that you are able to recall these things at any time after my departure.

**1 Corinthians 2:10-11 CSB**

Now God has revealed these things to us by the Spirit, since the Spirit searches everything, even the depths of God. [11] For who knows a person's thoughts except his spirit within him? In the same way, no one knows the thoughts of God except the Spirit of God.

**Staying Grounded in Truth**

The Apostle Peter knows that his time on earth is short. He is writing to the believers in Christ of his day, but his words also speak to us so that we will not forget the truths that he saw personally and learned at the feet of Jesus. Peter stressed how important it is to develop Christian character, as did other writers of the New Testament.

Why would the Apostle Peter need to wake believers up to remembrance? Because it is easy to become entangled in this world and forget that we have been called to be children of God. The Apostle Paul also warned us about the ways of the world and how the things of Christ would be considered foolishness by the world.

Let anyone sit in a classroom of higher education, and sooner or later they may encounter ideas that treat our faith as foolishness. Education and the pursuit of knowledge are not wrong; they can be good and necessary. But we live in a world where some believe that science, human knowledge, and education alone will lead the human race to greater heights. The cross is foolishness to those who are perishing and have not accepted the call of the Lord. Some may react to Christians as though we are ignoring reason and holding to the beliefs of foolish children, while others may respond with antagonism or even hatred.

But we who have had our eyes opened by God's Spirit know the truth. It is easy, however, to become trapped again by the things of the world and begin to drift from our faith.

Jesus promises when two or more are gathered in His name, that he is among us. Worship with other believers is so important. Sometimes I have a very rough week with health problems and teaching in my retirement age. The world sometimes feels like it is constantly against me. Then the miracle happens. I walk into church, sit down, and worship. The sounds of the world grow dim, and my faith is renewed as I corporately worship with other believers. I remember who I am in the Lord. The lies of the world did not win.

In 1 Corinthians 1:18 we find:

For the word of the cross is foolishness to those who are perishing, but it is the power of God to us who are being saved.

As we humbly worship, we are reminded of who God is and that we are in need of His gifts, especially the gift of forgiveness that comes through His Son Jesus. The world will say, “Who needs moral law? We are a law unto ourselves. We can make our own rules.” The word of the cross and the need for God is truly foolishness to those who reject Him. Pride can keep people from recognizing the truth and coming to God for salvation.

God has revealed truths to the Christian through the Holy Spirit that the world, apart from God, does not recognize or understand in the same way. This misunderstanding is propagated around the world and reaches the senses of a Christian almost daily. It is easy to be influenced by the rejection of the world and its constant message against God. But God gives more grace as we walk in the Spirit and surround ourselves with other believers. Let us not forget the truth that we have been shown since we first believed.

**Reflection Questions**

1. Do I view the things of God as the only truth, or do I mingle God's truths with the things of the world?

2. Do I make a conscious effort to have fellowship with other believers? Am I involved with the local church?

3. What ways has the world tried to draw me away from the truth of God this week?

**Prayer**

Heavenly Father,

Thank you for the salvation that is in Your Son Jesus and for allowing me to see the truth through Your Holy Spirit. May I resist the world that is constantly against You. Do not let me follow after foolish thoughts and beliefs, but help me stand firm in You. We know that education is not wrong, but let me hold to that which is true and reject that which is false. Teach me Your ways. Guide me on the path to righteousness. Help me to be part of a vibrant church where I remember who I am in You as the world tries to draw me away. Thank You for Your Son Jesus, and it is in His name we pray.

Amen.
