**DAY 16 – WONDER OF THE FATHER**

**Scripture**

**Deuteronomy 10:12 (CSB)**

"And now, Israel, what does the LORD your God ask of you except to fear the LORD your God by walking in all his ways, to love him, and to worship the LORD your God with all your heart and all your soul?"

**Fear or Wonder**

In Hebrew, there are two key words often translated as fear. The first, pachad, describes a paralyzing dread — the kind of fear that makes a person tremble before danger or punishment. This is what most of us imagine when we read “fear of the Lord.”

When I was growing up, there were church services where the pastor would yell and shout about damnation for those who did not believe in Jesus. There are verses that speak to judgment, of course — but the result in my young heart was a constant fear of doing something wrong in the sight of a demanding God. It left me anxious, unsure, and always bracing for punishment. That kind of fear is a precarious place to live.

But over the years, I’ve come to know a different side of the Father — a loving side. When we see a Father who holds out His hands so we can truly live — really live a good and meaningful life — our obedience shifts. We begin to follow Him not out of terror, but out of love from the heart.

The word for fear in Deuteronomy 10 is not this dreadful fear that drives us away. The word here is yirah, which carries a very different meaning. Yirah is awe, reverence, wonder. It is not about cringing before God — it is about being overwhelmed by His greatness, His beauty, and His love.

It is hard to truly love someone you are terrified of. The Father does not call us into dread, but into fellowship. The yirah kind of “fear” draws us closer, filling us with gratitude and a desire to walk in His ways.

To live in yirah is to stand amazed:
—to see God’s majesty in creation,
—His mercy in forgiveness,
—and His faithfulness in every moment of our lives.

It is to be humbled by His power and comforted by His nearness.

So the question for us becomes:
Do we live with pachad — fear that pushes us away?
Or with yirah — wonder that pulls us closer?

**Reflection Questions**

1. When I think of 'fearing' God, do I picture dread or reverent awe?

2. How can I cultivate a deeper sense of wonder and gratitude toward the Father in my daily life?

3. In what ways does recognizing God’s greatness lead me to love and trust Him more fully?

**Prayer**

Heavenly Father,

Thank You for revealing that Your 'fear' is not meant to frighten me but to fill me with awe and reverence. Teach me to stand in wonder before You — amazed by Your power, humbled by Your holiness, and grateful for Your mercy. Help me walk in Your ways out of love, not fear, and may my heart always be filled with the joy of knowing You as both Creator and Father.

Amen.
