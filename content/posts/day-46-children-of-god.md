**DAY 46 – CHILDREN OF GOD**

**Scripture**

**1 John 3:1-6 CSB**

See what great love the Father has given us that we should be called God's children-and we are! The reason the world does not know us is that it didn't know him. [2] Dear friends, we are God's children now, and what we will be has not yet been revealed. We know that when he appears, we will be like him because we will see him as he is. [3] And everyone who has this hope in him purifies himself just as he is pure. [4] Everyone who commits sin practices lawlessness; and sin is lawlessness. [5] You know that he was revealed so that he might take away sins, and there is no sin in him. [6] Everyone who remains in him does not sin; everyone who sins has not seen him or known him.

**Mark 7:20-23 CSB**

And he said, "What comes out of a person is what defiles him. [21] For from within, out of people's hearts, come evil thoughts, sexual immoralities, thefts, murders, [22] adulteries, greed, evil actions, deceit, self-indulgence, envy, slander, pride, and foolishness. **Lawlessness of Sin**

Those who have Jesus as Lord are already children of God. How great and wonderful this is! We have a home. Many wander through life feeling as though they do not belong. We can be the person who is always picked last to play a game or shunned by those around us. We can be lied about by people who are more gifted with words than we are, who deceive others and take our place because we struggle to express what is in our heads. Our appearance might not be as beautiful as that of others, and we may be rejected because of it. But we can also be those who are blessed in life with physical blessings and are humble enough to care for those around us. It does not matter. If we are God's children, we have found a home—a home of love and a family that surrounds us.

When I would choose a pet, I often found myself bringing home an abused dog, one that many people would not pick. I could tell that some had been beaten or mistreated. They would have nightmares and whine in their sleep. For some, it took years, but with constant love, they slowly learned to accept love and feel safe in their new home. They found peace.

In some ways, this reminds me of what we find in God's family. We come to Him with our hurts, failures, and sins, and through His Son, Jesus Christ, we find forgiveness, love, and a place where we belong. We do not have to earn His love by measuring up to what others expect of us.

Those outside the family of God may not understand the grace we have received. This grace begins to bear fruit in our lives as we do good works out of love for God and through the guidance of the Holy Spirit. Because we have the hope of seeing Jesus and becoming like Him, we seek to purify ourselves and grow more like our Lord.

But deep down, we are still flawed people, and we are prone to stray. Our biggest problem is not simply that we live among flawed people or that temptations are waiting around every corner. It is not only that we live in a broken world. We must also examine our own hearts. Sinful desires rise within us and draw our attention toward things that are not of God. If we follow those desires, we can begin to drift away from Him.

Our loving Father knows us and how easily we can be led away by the desire for sin in our hearts. Earlier in this letter, in 1 John 1:8–9, John reminds us that if we claim to have no sin, we deceive ourselves, but if we confess our sins, God is faithful and righteous to forgive us and cleanse us. Through His Son, Jesus Christ, we can humbly repent and turn back to Him when we fall short. Yet we are also called to grow in Christ. God has given us His Holy Spirit to give us victory over sin. As we confess our sins and turn away from them, He gives us the strength to overcome sinful desires and become more like His Son, Jesus.

John reminds us that we are God's children now, but what we will become has not yet been fully revealed. We will be like the Lord when we see Him face to face. Until then, we continue growing in Christlikeness, relying on His grace and remembering the sacrifice He made for us when we fall short.

One sign that a person truly belongs to Christ is a changed life. This is not a life spent trying to earn Heaven through good works. We are already God's children through faith in Jesus Christ. Our desire to do what is right grows out of the new life God has given us, and He empowers us to become more like His Son. As we follow the guidance of the Holy Spirit, we should continue to grow in Christ and experience victory over sin.

**Reflection Questions**

1. How has my life changed since I first believed in Jesus? Am I continuing to grow more like Him?

2. Are there sins in my life that I need to confess and ask the Lord for strength to turn away from?

3. How does it make me feel to know that I am already a child of God and have an eternal home waiting for me?

**Prayer**

Heavenly Father,

What a wonderful gift it is to be called one of Your children. Thank You for this blessing. Help me always remember that I have a home in Heaven so that the temptations of this world will grow dim and no longer seem worth following. Let Your Holy Spirit work in my life, guide me, and help me become more like Your Son, Jesus, each day. Give me the hope of knowing that one day I will be like Him when I see Him as He is. Forgive me for the times I fall short. Help me turn away from sin and remain in Your truth. Let me treat those around me with grace and love, without partiality, remembering the love You have shown me. In Jesus' name I pray.

Amen.
