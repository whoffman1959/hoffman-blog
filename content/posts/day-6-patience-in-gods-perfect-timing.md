**DAY 6 – PATIENCE IN GOD'S PERFECT TIMING**

**Scripture**

**James 5:7 (CSB)**

"Therefore, brothers and sisters, be patient until the Lord’s coming. See how the farmer waits for the precious fruit of the earth and is patient with it until it receives the early and the late rains."

**Peter 3:9 (CSB)**

"The Lord does not delay his promise, as some understand delay, but is patient with you, not wanting any to perish but all to come to repentance."

**In the Father's Time**

I remember when I first became a Christian. I not only read the Bible—treasuring every word—but, like many new believers, I devoured books about the end times. I couldn’t wait to see Jesus. I was desperate for the fullness of this new life and looked eagerly for His return. There were countless books on the subject, each offering dates or timetables, each promising signs. I didn’t realize then that the modern understanding of the rapture did not take its current shape until the early 19th century, according to historians. There are many mysteries that we are not meant to understand.

But over time, God taught me something important: I was so focused on trying to interpret the end times that I wasn’t living in the present. My eyes were glued to the sky, but I was missing the needs right in front of me. And I wasn’t recognizing that nearly two people die every second around the world — for them, eternity has already begun.

God gently shifted my attention. He reminded me that today matters. The people in front of me matter. My walk with Him right now matters.

Throughout history, believers have looked at the world around them—wars, disasters, unrest—and wondered if Christ’s return was near. Century after century, the Lord’s coming has not unfolded as people expected.

But God’s timing is not like ours. His patience is not neglect — it is mercy. Every day He waits is another opportunity for someone to turn to Him, another moment for grace to reach a wandering heart.

James reminds us to wait like the farmer who trusts the rhythm of the seasons. The early and late rains come when they are meant to come; the harvest ripens only in its time. In the same way, God is working quietly and faithfully in human history—and in our personal lives—bringing His purpose to completion with perfect wisdom.

Our calling is to live patiently, faithfully, and with steady hope. Christ has already come into our hearts, and His Spirit is at work in us even now. Whether His final return comes in our lifetime or another, we can rest in this truth:

He is never late.
He is always loving.
And He is always perfectly on time.

**Reflection Questions**

1. What does it mean to trust God’s timing when the world feels uncertain or chaotic?

2. How can I cultivate patience and faith while waiting for God to fulfill His promises?

3. In what ways can I use this time—before Christ’s return—to share His love and grace with others?

**Prayer**

Lord of all time,

Thank You for Your patience with us. When the world feels restless and uncertain, help me to rest in Your promises. Teach me to wait with faith, to trust that every moment is held in Your hands, and to live each day as a reflection of Your love. May my heart be steadfast, my spirit gentle, and my hope unshakable as I look toward the day when all things are made new.

Amen.
