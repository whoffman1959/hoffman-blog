**DAY 82 – PRECIOUS THOUGHTS**

**Scripture**

**Psalm 139:13-18 CSB**

For it was you who created my inward parts; you knit me together in my mother's womb. [14] I will praise you because I have been remarkably and wondrously made. Your works are wondrous, and I know this very well. [15] My bones were not hidden from you when I was made in secret, when I was formed in the depths of the earth. [16] Your eyes saw me when I was formless; all my days were written in your book and planned before a single one of them began. [17] God, how precious your thoughts are to me; how vast their sum is! [18] If I counted them, they would outnumber the grains of sand; when I wake up, I am still with you.

**Formed by His Hands**

There are times in life when we feel beaten and abandoned. We look at others who seem to have everything together, living happy and successful lives. Yet we often see only what they allow us to see. We do not know what they have suffered in the past, what struggles they may be facing today, or what difficulties tomorrow might bring. Everyone carries burdens that are not always visible to those around them.

In James 1:12, we are reminded that those who endure trials and remain faithful will receive the crown of life God has promised to those who love Him. Knowing this, how important it is that we live with compassion and love, allowing the light of Christ to shine into the lives of those who are struggling, even when we are facing difficulties ourselves.

Jesus gave us a beautiful example of such love. During His earthly ministry, He often grew tired and withdrew to quiet places to pray to His Heavenly Father. He once spoke of having nowhere to lay His head, yet He continued teaching, healing, and caring for those who came to Him. As God's only Son, He willingly gave Himself for our salvation. His love was not merely spoken; it was demonstrated through His life and sacrifice.

When life becomes especially difficult, we can find comfort in knowing that we are loved by God. David expresses this beautifully in today's psalm. He marvels at how carefully God formed him in his mother's womb and how completely God knew him before he ever took his first breath. Although David was writing about his own relationship with the Lord, his words remind us that each human life is known and wonderfully made by our Creator.

Consider the miracle of a child's birth. A mother endures the pain of labor, but when she holds her newborn child, that suffering gives way to joy. Those gathered around see a precious new life, a wonderful gift from God. Yet long before anyone held that child, God was already aware of the life developing within the mother's womb. David reminds us that nothing about our existence is hidden from our Heavenly Father.

The same God who knew us before we were born knows the trials we face today. His knowledge of our days does not mean we will be spared every hardship, but it assures us that our lives are never beyond His sight. Even in our darkest moments, we can find evidence of His love, sometimes through an unexpected kindness, an answered prayer, or someone who reaches out when we need encouragement most.

David closes this passage by marveling at the preciousness and vastness of God's thoughts. He compares their number to the grains of sand, more than he could ever count. What a beautiful reminder that our Heavenly Father is never too busy to know us or too distant to hear our prayers.

People may disappoint us, circumstances may change, and the road ahead may become difficult. But the God who formed us has not forgotten us. Even when we cannot see the way forward, we can trust the loving Father who sees us, knows us, and remains with us.

**Reflection Questions**

1. When you look at the people around you, do you remember that they may be carrying struggles you cannot see? How can you show someone the compassion of Christ today?

2. What does it mean to you to know that God formed you, knew you before your birth, and is aware of every difficulty you face?

3. When you have felt discouraged or alone, have there been moments when you recognized God's care through an answered prayer, an unexpected kindness, or the encouragement of another person?

**Prayer**

Heavenly Father,

Thank You for knowing us and loving us even before we took our first breath. You formed us with such wonderful care, and nothing in our lives is hidden from You. Forgive us when we become so concerned with our own troubles that we fail to recognize the struggles of others. Help us to show the compassion and kindness of Your Son, Jesus Christ, to those who are hurting. When life becomes difficult and we feel alone, remind us that You have not forgotten us. Open our eyes to the blessings and quiet reminders of Your love that surround us each day. Give us the strength to trust You, even when we cannot see the road ahead. In Jesus' precious name we pray.

Amen.
