**DAY 43 – FAMILY OF BELIEVERS**

**Scripture**

**1 John 2:12-17 CSB**

I am writing to you, little children, since your sins have been forgiven on account of his name. [13] I am writing to you, fathers, because you have come to know the one who is from the beginning. I am writing to you, young men, because you have conquered the evil one. [14] I have written to you, children, because you have come to know the Father. I have written to you, fathers, because you have come to know the one who is from the beginning. I have written to you, young men, because you are strong, God's word remains in you, and you have conquered the evil one. [15] Do not love the world or the things in the world. If anyone loves the world, the love of the Father is not in him. [16] For everything in the world-the lust of the flesh, the lust of the eyes, and the pride in one's possessions-is not from the Father, but is from the world. [17] And the world with its lust is passing away, but the one who does the will of God remains forever.

**Heaven in Mind**

As we walk this road of life, we have two directions that constantly battle for our affection. One is living horizontally. This is when we live and walk after the things of this world. All the things the world offers, both good and bad, can become little gods in our lives. We run after the things of the world, maybe occasionally turning our eyes toward God, but our main focus remains on worldly things and we become satisfied there.

The other way of living is vertically. This is when our eyes are looking toward Heaven. The Word of God is paramount in our hearts, and we desire to live our lives pleasing God, serving others rather than simply serving ourselves. We still live in this world and enjoy the good things God has given us, but they are not where we place our hope. We find this truth in our reflection today.

John is writing to a family of believers to remind them of these things. He writes to assure them that they are already in Christ. Their sins have been forgiven on account of His name. They have come to know the Father and the One who is from the beginning. God's Word remains in them, and they have conquered the evil one. There were those who would lead believers away from the truth, causing them to question what they had been taught and whether they truly understood the things of God. John reassures them that they already know the truth. They do not need some hidden or special knowledge that someone else claims they must have. Their forgiveness and hope are found in Jesus Christ.

John addresses children, fathers, and young men. He may be speaking of different ages within the family of believers, but his words can also remind us of different levels of spiritual maturity. Those who have walked with the Lord for many years have had time to grow in their knowledge of Christ and His Word, while younger believers may still have many questions as their faith grows. Yet age alone does not determine spiritual maturity. An older person may be new to the faith, while a younger person may have followed Christ for many years.

John has something encouraging to say to each of them. This should remind us that wherever we are in our walk with Christ, we are part of the same family of believers. Some of us have walked with the Lord for most of our lives, while others are just beginning. We can learn from those who have walked before us, encourage those who are younger in the faith, and continue growing together in our knowledge of Christ. None of us has reached the point where there is nothing more to learn.

John then repeats himself. Why? When Scripture repeats something, it should cause us to pay attention. These believers needed to be reminded of what they already possessed. Their sins were forgiven. They knew the Father. They knew the One who is from the beginning. God's Word remained in them. They did not need someone to come along and tell them that their faith was in error because they lacked some special understanding. They already had the truth that had been given to them through Jesus Christ and His apostles.

John now turns his words toward how this family of believers should live. The believers of John's day faced the attractions of the world just as we do today. We can easily begin living horizontally, allowing our desires and passions to become focused on things that draw us away from God, hurt our Christian walk, and tarnish our testimony of the life that God has given us through Jesus.
John puts it plainly: the lust of the flesh, the lust of the eyes, and pride in what we possess are all passing away. There is nothing wrong with enjoying the good things God has given us, but they should never become the focus of our lives. Possessions, accomplishments, pleasures, and even our time here on earth are temporary. The things of God are eternal.

John reminds us that we are part of God's family. Our sins are forgiven through Jesus Christ, we know the Father, and we have His Word. We do not need to spend our lives searching for some hidden truth or something more than Christ. Instead, we can keep our eyes focused on Him and the things of Heaven. We still have responsibilities, families, work, and things to enjoy in this life, but they are not our final destination. The things of this world are passing away, but what we have in Christ is eternal. We can live each day knowing who we belong to and where our true home is.

**Reflection Questions**

1. Am I looking toward the things of Heaven, or does my passion sometimes turn toward the things of this world? Am I trying to get everything I can from this life before I pass on to meet Jesus face to face?

2. Am I grounded in the foundational Christian beliefs taught by the apostles and passed down by the believers who came before me? Do I know that my salvation is found in Jesus Christ, or am I searching for some special knowledge that I think I must have?

3. What changes took place in my life when I first came to believe? Is my faith continuing to grow so that I am becoming more like Jesus?

**Prayer**

Heavenly Father,

Thank You that the message of eternal life is simple. We do not need special knowledge to be saved, but faith in Your Son, Jesus Christ, who died on the cross for our sins. Let us always focus on Your Son as the way to have fellowship with You and the forgiveness of our sins. Keep us from the things of this world that would draw us away from You, and protect us from the evil one and his lies. Thank You for being our God and for keeping us as Your people. Help us to live each day with Heaven in mind. In Your Son's name we pray.

Amen.
