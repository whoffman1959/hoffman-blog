**DAY 1 – LIKE A SMALL CHILD**

**Scripture**

**Mark 10:13–15 (CSB)**

"People were bringing little children to him in order that he might touch them, but the disciples rebuked them. [14] When Jesus saw it, he was indignant and said to them, “Let the little children come to me. Don’t stop them, because the kingdom of God belongs to such as these. [15] Truly I tell you, whoever does not receive the kingdom of God like a little child will never enter it.”

**Faith of a Child**

We just welcomed a new grandbaby into the family. What a gift. This little one does everything a baby does — cries, eats, sleeps, needs diapers changed, doctor visits scheduled, and a whole list of small but beautiful needs. But what captures me most is the way a baby looks at the world: wide-eyed, trusting, completely dependent. When our grandbaby looks up at us, sometimes with a smile and sometimes with tears, those tiny arms always reach out with the same expectation — someone will hold me, someone will help me, someone will care for me.

This, Jesus says, is what faith looks like.

When the disciples tried to send the children away, Jesus stopped them. He was indignant — not because He was angry at the disciples, but because they were missing something essential. Children are not an interruption to the Kingdom; they are a living picture of it.

A child doesn’t question whether a parent will come. A child doesn’t analyze love or measure worthiness. A child simply trusts. And when they stumble, they reach upward, confident that someone stronger will lift them.

This is the kind of faith Jesus invites us to have — a childlike trust in the Father’s goodness. Not a naïve or simplistic faith, but a humble one. A faith that says, “I can’t do life on my own. I need my Father.”

If God did not withhold His own Son for us, how much more will He walk with us, provide for us, and carry us through the moments when we feel small or overwhelmed?

The Kingdom of God is not something we earn. It is something we receive — the way a child receives the embrace of a parent, arms open, trusting, resting in love.

**Reflection Questions**

1. Do I approach God with a childlike trust, or do I try to prove myself worthy first?

2. In what areas of my life do I struggle to simply rest in God’s care, like a child rests in a parent’s arms?

3. How can I cultivate a heart that receives God’s Kingdom with wonder, humility, and faith?

**Prayer**

Father,

Thank You for showing us that Your Kingdom belongs to those with childlike faith. Teach me to trust You more deeply, to lay down my pride, and to receive Your love without hesitation. Help me to come to You with open hands and an open heart, confident that You will never turn me away.

Amen.
