**DAY 83 – WE ARE LOVED**

**Scripture**

**Psalm 103:8-13 CSB**

The LORD is compassionate and gracious, slow to anger and abounding in faithful love. [9] He will not always accuse us or be angry forever. [10] He has not dealt with us as our sins deserve or repaid us according to our iniquities. [11] For as high as the heavens are above the earth, so great is his faithful love toward those who fear him. [12] As far as the east is from the west, so far has he removed our transgressions from us. [13] As a father has compassion on his children, so the LORD has compassion on those who fear him.

**We are Forgiven**

Growing up as a child, I remember the love of my parents. No matter what I did, they had a forgiving love for me. I was special to them simply because I was their child. This did not mean I escaped correction when I did something wrong. I learned to respect my parents and understand that my actions had consequences.

Sometimes they would tell me that correcting me hurt them more than it hurt me. I may not have believed that at the time, but as I grew older, I began to understand. Even when I was at my worst, their love remained.

We have a loving Heavenly Father whose compassion and mercy are greater than anything we can imagine. Our Lord is gracious, patient, and abounding in faithful love. Just as my parents forgave me, our Heavenly Father offers us forgiveness through His Son, Jesus Christ. David tells us in this Psalm that God has not treated us as our sins deserve. Those who turn to Him in repentance and faith can receive forgiveness and a new life. As high as the heavens are above the earth, so great is His faithful love. As far as the east is from the west, so far has He removed our transgressions from us.

David also tells us that the Lord has compassion on those who fear Him. This is not the dreadful fear of someone waiting for the hand of an executioner. It is a deep and reverent awe, a recognition of God's holiness, power, and authority. We love Him, respect Him, and desire to follow His ways, just as a child who loves his parents learns to respect their guidance and correction.

This same loving nature was revealed to Moses centuries before David wrote these words. In Exodus 34:6–7, the Lord passed before Moses and proclaimed Himself compassionate and gracious, slow to anger, and abounding in faithful love and truth. He spoke of His willingness to forgive iniquity, rebellion, and sin, while also declaring that He would not leave guilt unpunished. God's mercy does not mean that sin is unimportant. His holiness and justice remain, but through Jesus Christ, our Savior, we find the forgiveness that we could never earn on our own.

Many people continue to live under the weight of things they have done in the past. They replay painful memories, wishing they could return to an earlier time and make different choices. Perhaps they have hurt someone they loved, spoken words they cannot take back, or made decisions that changed the direction of their lives. Although we may still face the earthly consequences of those choices, we do not have to remain prisoners of guilt.

When we come to Christ in repentance and faith, we can trust His promise of forgiveness.

Think of the love of a father who welcomes his child home after that child has wandered away. The mistakes are real, and some consequences may remain, but the father's love has not disappeared. How much greater is the mercy of our Heavenly Father, who loved us enough to send His only Son to bring us back to Him?

We cannot change yesterday, but through God's grace, we can begin today with a forgiven heart.

Our Heavenly Father knows our failures, yet He invites us to come to Him. His love is not something we earn by being perfect. It is a precious gift, made known to us through Jesus Christ.

**Reflection Questions**

1. When you remember the love and correction you received growing up, how does that help you understand the compassion of your Heavenly Father?

2. Are there mistakes from your past that you continue to carry, even though you have sought God's forgiveness? What would it mean for you to trust more fully in His mercy?

3. God's compassion toward us is greater than we can imagine. Is there someone in your life who needs to experience that same patience, forgiveness, and love through you?

**Prayer**

Heavenly Father,

Thank You for Your wonderful compassion, patience, and faithful love. We know that we have fallen short of Your holiness, yet You have offered us forgiveness through Your Son, Jesus Christ. Forgive us for the mistakes we have made and for the times we have failed to follow Your ways. Help us to release the guilt of sins we have brought before You in repentance and to trust in the forgiveness You have promised. Teach us to show the same patience and compassion to others that You have shown to us. When memories of the past trouble our hearts, remind us that through Christ we can begin anew. Thank You for loving us as Your children and for never turning away those who come to You in faith. In Jesus' precious name we pray.

Amen.
