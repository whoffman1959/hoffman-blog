**DAY 21 – NOT BY WORKS**

**Scripture**

**Galatians 3:5–6 (CSB)**

So then, does God give you the Spirit and work miracles among you by your doing the works of the law? Or is it by believing what you heard- [6] just like Abraham who believed God, and it was credited to him for righteousness?

**Galatians 3:11 (CSB)**

Now it is clear that no one is justified before God by the law, because the righteous will live by faith.

**By Faith Alone**

Someone once said, “If you served and worshiped the Father for a thousand years, you would be no more saved than the person who takes their first step of faith today.”

How easily we forget this. We slip into thinking our devotion earns God’s favor. “Look what I’ve done, Lord,” we say. “I go to church, I give to the poor, I pray every day.” Then we glance at someone who wanders in from the street, broken and whispering, “God, help me.”

After the service, others gather around that person, offering love and encouragement. Before long, that same person confesses faith in Jesus and becomes a new brother or sister in Christ. Yet, pride still lingers in us — that subtle thought, “I’ve been here longer; I’ve done more.”

Oh, the depth of our pride.

God’s holiness is so perfect that even our best deeds are like filthy rags before Him. We could give away everything, face persecution, and spend our lives in constant prayer — and it would never be enough to earn salvation. The law shows us this truth clearly: we all fall short.

But the Gospel brings freedom. Jesus Christ, the Father’s only Son, was beaten, mocked, and crucified — not for the righteous, but for sinners. His perfect obedience covers our failure. Through His sacrifice, the Father declares us righteous by faith, not by our efforts.

We are not saved because of who we are or what we do.
We are saved because of who He is — and what He has done.
All who believe in Jesus and place their trust in Him are welcomed into the Father’s family.

Grace is the great equalizer.

**Reflection Questions**

1. Have I ever found myself believing that my good works make me more deserving of God’s favor?

2. How can I remind myself daily that salvation is a gift of grace through faith — not something I can earn?

3. In what ways can I show grace and humility toward those who are new in their faith?

**Prayer**

Heavenly Father,

Thank You for the gift of grace — undeserved, unearned, yet freely given through Your Son. Forgive me for the pride that sometimes hides in my heart, for the moments when I think my efforts make me worthy of Your love. Remind me daily that Jesus paid it all, that His cross is enough, and that faith in Him alone brings salvation. Help me extend that same mercy to others, rejoicing whenever someone new finds life in You. Let my heart rest in the finished work of Christ.

Amen.
