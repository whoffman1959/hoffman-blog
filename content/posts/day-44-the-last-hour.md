**DAY 44 – THE LAST HOUR**

**Scripture**

**1 John 2:18-23 CSB**

Children, it is the last hour. And as you have heard that antichrist is coming, even now many antichrists have come. By this we know that it is the last hour. [19] They went out from us, but they did not belong to us; for if they had belonged to us, they would have remained with us. However, they went out so that it might be made clear that none of them belongs to us. [20] But you have an anointing from the Holy One, and all of you know the truth. [21] I have not written to you because you don't know the truth, but because you do know it, and because no lie comes from the truth. [22] Who is the liar, if not the one who denies that Jesus is the Christ? This one is the antichrist: the one who denies the Father and the Son. [23] No one who denies the Son has the Father; he who confesses the Son has the Father as well.

**Confess the Son**

Many people recognize that humans can be the cause of their own destruction on this planet. This became a greater concern with the development of the atomic bomb and its use in 1945, when the United States dropped atomic bombs on Hiroshima and Nagasaki.

To give meaning to these concerns, scientists developed what is called the Doomsday Clock. It has been maintained since 1947 and is a symbol of how close humanity may be to a global catastrophe of its own making. In January 2026, the clock was set at 85 seconds to midnight. Midnight represents a catastrophic event that could threaten the world as we know it.

Scripture also gives us warnings about the last days, but we will not know the day or time when the clock strikes midnight. The Apostle John tells us that it is the last hour and that it has been since his day. He reminds us that we have heard the antichrist is coming, but he also warns us that many antichrists have already come. The coming antichrist is a serious warning for us to consider, but the danger is not only something in the future. It was already present among the believers in John's day, just as it is among us today.

Paul also warns us in 2 Thessalonians chapter 2 about the man of lawlessness. He will come, and oppose God and exalt himself, seeking worship that belongs to God alone. This will be a mark of the end times, and Paul tells us that the Lord Jesus will destroy him at His coming.

Scripture warns us that deception and opposition to Christ are serious matters. Some may even lift themselves up as though they could take the place of Christ. Yet John brings his warning close to home. He is concerned about people who had been among the believers but had left them and were now denying the truth about Jesus Christ.

An antichrist is any person, false teacher, or force that denies or opposes Jesus Christ, or attempts to take His place. John warns us that this can happen even within the church.

The sad thing is that, in John’s day, these people had once been part of the Christian community. John tells us that they went out from among the believers because they did not truly belong to them. Their departure revealed that they had not held to the foundational message about Jesus Christ that they had first heard through the apostles. They were now teaching things that denied the truth about Jesus Christ and could lead others away from the salvation found in Him.

John then tells us plainly how to recognize the antichrists he is warning about. They deny that Jesus is the Christ. They deny the Father and the Son. We cannot claim to know the Father while rejecting His Son, because God's plan of redemption is found in Jesus Christ. Many people try to create their own ways to be saved, but we cannot set aside the way God has given us and expect to have fellowship with Him.

This warning is just as important for us today. There are still people who claim to speak for God while teaching things that deny who Jesus truly is. Some may use Christian words while giving them a different meaning. Others may tell us that the truth we first learned about Jesus is not enough and that we need some special understanding to know God. We need to examine what we hear against the Word of God and remain grounded in the truth about His Son.

But John does not leave the believers with only a warning. He reminds them that they have an anointing from the Holy One and that they know the truth. God has given us His Holy Spirit, who helps us understand His Word and remain in the truth. We do not have to live in fear that someone will come along with a hidden message that we must discover before we can belong to God. Our hope is in Jesus Christ.

John's words should cause us to examine our own faith. Do we truly confess Jesus as the Christ, the Son of God? Are we trusting in His death on the cross for the forgiveness of our sins, or are we trying to find another way to God? Are we remaining in the truth that has been given to us through His Word?

We do not know when the final day will come. The Doomsday Clock reminds us that human actions can bring great destruction, but Scripture reminds us of something even more important: our lives are in God's hands, and we need to be prepared to meet Him. We can live each day trusting in His Son, remaining in His Word, and sharing with others the forgiveness and eternal life that are found in Jesus Christ.

**Reflection Questions**

1. What are some teachings in the world today that deny who Jesus truly is? How can I use God's Word to recognize them?

2. When I hear warnings about the last days, do they cause me to become fearful, or do they remind me to remain in Christ and share His message of salvation with others?

3. Am I trusting in Jesus Christ alone for the forgiveness of my sins, or am I allowing other teachings to draw me away from the truth I first believed?

**Prayer**

Heavenly Father,

We know that time has continued to pass since the days of the Apostle John. We do not know when the final day and judgment will come, but we know that day is drawing nearer. Let us as believers take our responsibility seriously and tell others about the grace and forgiveness given through Your Son, Jesus Christ, that lead to eternal life. Keep us from following those who would lead us away from Your Son. Keep us centered in the faith and build us up through Your Holy Spirit. Let Your truth always be the foundation of our lives, and do not let us be led astray. Keep us from trying to create our own path to salvation. In Jesus' name we pray.

Amen.
