**DAY 89 – A PLACE PREPARED FOR YOU**

**Scripture**

**John 14:1-6 CSB**

"Don't let your heart be troubled. Believe in God; believe also in me. [2] In my Father's house are many rooms. If it were not so, would I have told you that I am going to prepare a place for you? [3] If I go away and prepare a place for you, I will come again and take you to myself, so that where I am you may be also. [4] You know the way to where I am going." [5] "Lord," Thomas said, "we don't know where you're going. How can we know the way?" [6] Jesus told him, "I am the way, the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father except through me.

**Our Eternal Home**

There is something comforting about coming home after a long journey. Perhaps you have been traveling for days, sleeping in unfamiliar beds, eating meals in restaurants, and living out of a suitcase. You have seen wonderful things and met interesting people, but eventually you begin to long for home. You want to sit in your favorite chair, enjoy the familiar surroundings, and rest among those you love. There is no place quite like home.

I sometimes think about our journey through this world in much the same way. We spend our lives building homes, raising families, working to provide for those we love, and enjoying the blessings our Heavenly Father has given us. Yet no matter how comfortable we become, there is always something reminding us that this world is not our permanent home.

As the years pass, we begin to notice things changing. Children grow up and begin families of their own. Dear friends and loved ones pass from this life. Our bodies grow older, and things that once seemed so easy become difficult. The world around us changes, sometimes so quickly that we hardly recognize it. We begin to understand that the things we have worked so hard to build cannot last forever.

But Jesus has given us a wonderful promise.

In John chapter 14, we find Jesus speaking to His disciples during the final evening before His crucifixion. He had already told them that He would be leaving. Imagine how troubling those words must have been. These men had walked with Jesus, listened to His teaching, witnessed His miracles, and left behind much of their former lives to follow Him. Now the One they loved was telling them He was going away.

They did not yet understand everything that was about to happen. Within hours, Jesus would be arrested. He would suffer, be condemned, and die upon a cross. Their world was about to be turned upside down.

Yet in those final hours, Jesus was thinking about comforting them.

He told them not to let their hearts be troubled. They were to believe in God and believe also in Him. Then He spoke of His Father's house, where there were many rooms, and assured them that He was going to prepare a place for them. He promised that He would come again and take them to Himself so that they could be where He is.

What a precious promise from our Savior!

Jesus was not speaking of some temporary shelter where His followers might rest for a little while. He was speaking of an eternal home in the presence of our Heavenly Father. A place where the troubles of this world will no longer follow us, where sin and death will no longer separate us from those we love in Christ, and where we will enjoy fellowship with our Lord forever.

I sometimes wonder what that first moment in Heaven will be like. Will we stand in silent wonder at the beauty surrounding us? Will we fall to our knees in worship? Will we recognize loved ones who have gone before us in the Lord? Scripture does not answer every question we might have, but it gives us something far more important. Jesus has promised that we will be with Him.

And that is what makes Heaven such a wonderful treasure. Not streets of gold or beautiful surroundings, but the presence of our Lord and Savior, who loved us enough to give His life for us.

Thomas, however, was still confused. He told Jesus that they did not know where He was going, so how could they possibly know the way?

Jesus answered with one of the most important truths in all of Scripture. He declared that He is the way, the truth, and the life, and that no one comes to the Father except through Him.

Notice that Jesus did not give Thomas a list of religious accomplishments or good works he must complete to reach Heaven. He pointed Thomas to Himself.

This is the wonderful hope of the gospel. Our eternal home is not something we can earn through our own efforts. We cannot purchase our way into God's presence with money, good deeds, or religious traditions. Jesus Christ has opened the way through His death and resurrection. Through faith in Him, we receive forgiveness and the promise of eternal life.

Until that day comes, we still have a journey to complete. There will be beautiful mornings filled with laughter and joy, and there will be difficult nights when sorrow weighs heavily upon our hearts. We may experience sickness, disappointment, and the loss of things we once held dear. But none of these troubles can erase the promise Jesus has made to those who belong to Him.

One day, our journey through this world will end. We will leave behind the possessions we have gathered, the homes we have built, and the worries that occupied so much of our time. But we will not be traveling into the unknown without hope.

Our Savior has already gone before us.

And when that day comes, what a wonderful joy it will be to discover that the place we have longed for throughout our lives is finally our home.

**Reflection Questions**

1. Jesus told His disciples not to let their hearts be troubled. When you think about the uncertainties of life and the future, how does His promise of an eternal home bring peace to your heart?

2. We spend much of our lives gathering possessions and building comfortable homes. Have there been times when the things of this world became more important to you than your relationship with Jesus Christ?

3. Jesus declared that He is the way, the truth, and the life. How does knowing that your salvation rests in Him, rather than your own accomplishments, strengthen your faith and give you hope for eternity?

**Prayer**

Heavenly Father,

Thank You for the wonderful promise of an eternal home in Your presence. We are grateful that Your Son, Jesus Christ, has opened the way for us through His sacrifice upon the cross. Forgive us when we become so occupied with the things of this world that we forget where our true home awaits. Comfort those who are grieving, strengthen those who are struggling, and give peace to those who are fearful of what tomorrow may bring. Help us to remember that the troubles of this life are temporary, but Your love and promises are eternal. May we spend our remaining days growing closer to You, loving others, and sharing the hope we have found in Jesus. And when our journey through this world is complete, may we rejoice in being forever with our Lord and Savior. In Jesus' precious name we pray.

Amen.
