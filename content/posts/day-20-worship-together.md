**DAY 20 – WORSHIP TOGETHER**

**Scripture**

**Psalm 63:1–2 (CSB)**

God, you are my God; I eagerly seek you. I thirst for you; my body faints for you in a land that is dry, desolate, and without water. [2] So I gaze on you in the sanctuary to see your strength and your glory.

**Hebrews 10:25 (CSB)**

not neglecting to gather together, as some are in the habit of doing,
but encouraging each other, and all the more as you see the day approaching.

**Fellowship**

The psalm was written by David while he was in the wilderness of Judah — a dry, barren place that mirrored his longing for the presence of God.

It’s easy to drift from church. We tell ourselves, “It’s just a building. I can walk with God in the woods.” But most of the time, we don’t. Life gets busy. We feel hurt by someone at church. We’re tired and think, “I’ll just rest today.” Over time, the habit of worship fades, and we find ourselves wandering — spiritually dry, like David in the desert.

Then one day, something stirs. We remember what we’ve lost — the joy of worship, the comfort of belonging, the power of shared prayer. We thirst again for God’s presence, and we return to His people.

When we come back, often we’re welcomed on a day of communion — the Lord’s Supper, the Eucharist — the sacred remembrance of what Christ did for us. We examine our hearts and receive the bread and cup with humility and gratitude, remembering that Jesus is our Paschal Lamb.

Just as the Jewish people ate the Paschal lamb in obedience to God’s command — a sacrifice marking their faith so that they would not suffer as the Egyptians did — so we remember that Jesus is the lamb of God. Christ’s obedience and sacrifice brought our deliverance.
In that moment, surrounded by fellow believers — imperfect but redeemed — we remember who we are and whose we are.
We are home.

**Reflection Questions**

1. What keeps me from gathering with other believers — and how can I overcome it?

2. How has worship with others encouraged or strengthened me in the past?

3. When I take part in communion, how can I make it a deeper time of reflection and gratitude?

**Prayer**

Heavenly Father,

Thank You for calling me back when I drift away. Thank You for the gift of fellowship — the joy of worshiping You with others, of praying, singing, and growing together. Forgive me when I let excuses or hurt keep me from Your house. Renew my desire to seek You in community, to serve and encourage others, and to remember the sacrifice of Your Son with awe and thankfulness.
Draw me close to You — and to Your family.

Amen.
