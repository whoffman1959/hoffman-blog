**DAY 39 – FROM THE BEGINNING**

**Scripture**

**1 John 1:1-5 CSB**

What was from the beginning, what we have heard, what we have seen with our eyes, what we have observed and have touched with our hands, concerning the word of life- [2] that life was revealed, and we have seen it and we testify and declare to you the eternal life that was with the Father and was revealed to us- [3] what we have seen and heard we also declare to you, so that you may also have fellowship with us; and indeed our fellowship is with the Father and with his Son, Jesus Christ. [4] We are writing these things so that our joy may be complete. [5] This is the message we have heard from him and declare to you: God is light, and there is absolutely no darkness in him.

**Matthew 25:24-25 CSB**

"The man who had received one talent also approached and said, 'Master, I know you. You're a harsh man, reaping where you haven't sown and gathering where you haven't scattered seed. [25] So I was afraid and went off and hid your talent in the ground. See, you have what is yours.'

**Revealed to Us**

As we focus on the letter of First John, we find that John points to the fact that we, as God's children, are members of the same family, and there are certain similarities that we share—a growing likeness to our Father in Heaven. It is as if we sit down on the couch and look through a photo album of our earthly family. In the pictures, we see resemblances that we can point out in one another.

There are things we share as Christians. We acknowledge Jesus Christ as our Lord and Savior. We love God and one another. We desire to walk according to God's commands, grow in our knowledge of Him, and turn from sin in our lives. Throughout his letter, John will point to these things as signs that we truly belong to the Father. Later, John tells his readers that he has written so that those who believe in the name of the Son of God may know that they have eternal life.

As with the letters of Peter, false teaching had brought confusion among believers. John speaks of people who had gone out from among them and warns about those who denied the truth about Jesus Christ. False teaching can cause believers to question what they have been taught and even cause them to doubt that they belong to God.

John writes to assure these Christians of the truth they had received. They did not need some new secret teaching that replaced the gospel they had already heard. They needed to remain in the truth about Jesus Christ that had been proclaimed to them from the beginning.

John starts this letter by saying that he personally knew the Lord. He had heard Jesus, seen Him with his own eyes, observed Him, and touched Him with his hands. He was there during the earthly ministry of God's Son and witnessed His miracles. He sat under the teaching of the Lord. He heard the parables and was among the disciples when Jesus explained things they did not understand.

John is now testifying to what he had seen and heard concerning the Word of life. He declares these truths so that those who receive his testimony may share in fellowship with the believers—and, more importantly, understand that this fellowship is with God the Father and His Son, Jesus Christ.

Many misunderstand who God is. Some picture a vengeful God who is always ready to throw lightning bolts from His throne. There is truth in saying that God is Judge, but that is not the whole revelation of His character. God is also love, and God is light. He sent His only Son for us. Our Father in Heaven cares for His creation. If we are to grow in our likeness to our Father, we must know Him as He has revealed Himself. John tells us that God is light and there is absolutely no darkness in Him.

Through this one statement, John tells us something profound about the character of God. He is absolutely holy, righteous, and pure. There is no evil or darkness in Him. In His love, God provided His Son as the atoning sacrifice for our sins. God is light, and later in this letter we will see that God is love. Our growing love for God and for one another is one of the signs that we belong to His family.

In Matthew chapter 25, Jesus tells of a servant whose view of his master affected the way he served him. The servant who received one talent described his master as harsh. Because he was afraid, he buried what had been entrusted to him rather than faithfully using it. His understanding of his master affected his actions.

We can also develop a distorted understanding of our Father in Heaven. If we see God only as an angry taskmaster waiting for us to fail, fear can shape the way we relate to Him and serve others. But if we ignore His holiness and judgment, we have distorted His character in the other direction. We need to know God as He has revealed Himself: holy and just, but also compassionate, gracious, patient, and abounding in faithful love and truth. How we understand our Father in Heaven affects how we live before Him and how we serve others.

God revealed something of His character to Moses in Exodus chapter 34:

The LORD passed in front of him and proclaimed: The LORD-the LORD is a compassionate and gracious God, slow to anger and abounding in faithful love and truth, maintaining faithful love to a thousand generations, forgiving iniquity, rebellion, and sin. But he will not leave the guilty unpunished, bringing the consequences of the fathers' iniquity on the children and grandchildren to the third and fourth generation.

That is the God we serve. He is compassionate and gracious, slow to anger and abounding in faithful love and truth. He forgives iniquity, rebellion, and sin, yet He is also just. John adds another beautiful truth for us to remember: God is light, and there is absolutely no darkness in Him.

**Reflection Questions**

1.	How do I really view God? Do I know Him as “Abba,” my Father, or do I see Him only as a harsh taskmaster?

2.	Are there false teachings and beliefs being spread in the world today that could lead people away from the truth about Christ?

3.	Do I know deep in my heart that I truly belong to the family of God and have eternal life through Jesus Christ? 

**Prayer**

Heavenly Father,

Let me always remember that You are compassionate and gracious, slow to anger, and patient with me. Thank You for sending Your Son to be the sacrifice for my sins. Forgive me for the times that I have failed to reflect Your likeness. Let me always remember that You abound in faithful love and truth and that You forgive rebellion and sin. Let me truly know that You are light and that there is no darkness in You. Protect me from false teaching, and give me assurance through Your Word that I truly belong to You. Give me the courage to go out today and share with others the hope I have found in You. In Jesus' name I pray.

Amen.
