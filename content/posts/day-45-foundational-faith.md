**DAY 45 – FOUNDATIONAL FAITH**

**Scripture**

**1 John 2:24-29 CSB**

What you have heard from the beginning is to remain in you. If what you have heard from the beginning remains in you, then you will remain in the Son and in the Father. [25] And this is the promise that he himself made to us: eternal life. [26] I have written these things to you concerning those who are trying to deceive you. [27] As for you, the anointing you received from him remains in you, and you don't need anyone to teach you. Instead, his anointing teaches you about all things and is true and is not a lie; just as it has taught you, remain in him. [28] So now, little children, remain in him so that when he appears we may have confidence and not be ashamed before him at his coming. [29] If you know that he is righteous, you know this as well: Everyone who does what is right has been born of him.

**Apostles Teachings**

There was an English Christian monk, Bible historian, and scholar named Bede who lived from about AD 673 to AD 735. He is especially remembered for his writings on Scripture and the history of the early English church. In his commentary on 1 John, he wrote:

“Follow with all your heart that faith and that teaching which you have received from the apostles at the beginning of the church, for only this will make you partakers of divine grace.”

This is what the Apostle John has been saying throughout his letter. We are to remain in the truth that was given to us through Jesus Christ and His apostles.
Sometimes we make the mistake of thinking that those who lived before us did not have the understanding that we have today. We may dismiss their wisdom simply because they lived in another time. Yet the Holy Spirit was at work in the lives of believers long before we were born, just as He is at work in us today. We should pay special attention to the teachings of the apostles and what we can learn from those who learned directly from them and passed their teachings on to others.

We can also learn much from later Christian teachers, such as Bede. However, we should not accept everything someone has written simply because that person lived long ago, nor should we reject it without examining it. We have God's Word as our standard for truth, and the Holy Spirit helps us understand it. God has also given us teachers who can help us grow in our faith. What we do not need is someone claiming to have secret knowledge that changes the truth about Jesus Christ given to us through His apostles.

Bede also reminds us of what John says about the anointing we have received from the Holy One. We have the Holy Spirit dwelling within us, helping us understand God's Word and remain in the truth. We do not need the supposed secret knowledge of self-proclaimed spiritual teachers who have constructed their own teachings that do not agree with the words of Jesus and His apostles. The Holy Spirit is a gift of love from God, helping us remain grounded in the truth in a world filled with half-truths and lies.

John tells us to remain in Christ so that we may have confidence when He returns and not be ashamed before Him. There may be times when standing for Christ brings ridicule, rejection, or persecution. We may struggle with fear, but we can ask the Lord to give us the courage to remain faithful to Him. There is a serious difference between struggling to speak about our faith and choosing to reject Christ before the world. Jesus warned us about denying Him before others. Yet we also remember Peter, who denied Jesus three times and was later forgiven and restored. When we fail, we can turn back to the Lord in repentance and receive His forgiveness. Our confidence is not in our own strength but in remaining in Jesus Christ and trusting in His promise of eternal life.

John also reminds us of another precious truth: those who belong to God's family show it through the way they live. One sign that we truly belong to Him is that we grow in our desire to do what is right. There is a saying: Do the next right thing. Those who follow Jesus and are led by the Holy Spirit should have a growing desire to do what is right and turn away from the errors that surround them. We will not always get it right, but when we fail, we can turn to the Lord for forgiveness and ask Him to help us do better.

Doing what is right does not earn us a place in God's family. Rather, it is evidence of the new life He has given us through Jesus Christ. John calls those who follow Jesus his little children. This title of love reminds us that we belong to the same family, sharing in the blessings of believing in Jesus.

We are part of a family of believers that reaches back through the centuries. We can learn from those who have gone before us, encourage one another today, and continue growing together in our faith. We have received the truth about Jesus Christ, and we have His promise of eternal life. Let us remain in Him as we wait for His return.

**Reflection Questions**

1. Do I spend time in Bible study and prayer? Do I surround myself with other believers? Am I being fed by the Holy Spirit through these things so that I may remain in the truth?

2. How does it make me feel to know that I belong to a family of believers that reaches back through the centuries? Do I look forward to the day when we will all be together with the Lord in eternity?

3. Do I reject teachings from the past simply because they were written centuries ago, or do I take time to examine them against the truth of the Holy Bible?

**Prayer**

Heavenly Father,

Thank You for making us a family of believers. Let us hold to this precious gift when things are good and when things are hard for us. Give us the wisdom, courage, strength, and faith to stand for Your Son, Jesus, and not deny our faith when confronted by those in the world who do not understand the truth. Keep us from following after their ways, and help us resist falsehood, even when standing for the truth may cause us earthly pain for a short time. Guide us in all that we say and do. Keep our words filled with grace so that we can be a light of the gospel to others. Help us to remain in Your Son and look forward with confidence to the day when He returns. In the name of Your precious Son we pray.

Amen.
