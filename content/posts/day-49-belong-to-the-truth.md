**DAY 49 – BELONG TO THE TRUTH**

**Scripture**

**1 John 3:19-24 CSB**

This is how we will know that we belong to the truth and will reassure our hearts before him [20] whenever our hearts condemn us; for God is greater than our hearts, and he knows all things. [21] Dear friends, if our hearts don't condemn us, we have confidence before God [22] and receive whatever we ask from him because we keep his commands and do what is pleasing in his sight. [23] Now this is his command: that we believe in the name of his Son, Jesus Christ, and love one another as he commanded us. [24] The one who keeps his commands remains in him, and he in him. And the way we know that he remains in us is from the Spirit he has given us.

**Romans 8:31-33 CSB**

What, then, are we to say about these things? If God is for us, who is against us? [32] He did not even spare his own Son but gave him up for us all. How will he not also with him grant us everything? [33] Who can bring an accusation against God's elect? God is the one who justifies.

**Assurance**

One of the greatest gifts given to us to help guide our lives is our conscience. I remember my conscience screaming at me as a young child when I wanted to do something wrong. It seems that most of us have been given this gift from God to help us do what is right. When a person continually fights against their conscience, that inner voice can eventually become quiet. We have learned to ignore it so that we might do the things we want without feeling guilty.

I did this as I entered my twenties and early thirties, living for the things of this world. But God was still at work in my life, and through those misadventures, He called me to Himself. He gave me the Holy Spirit to guide me and renewed my conscience. Yet a quiet conscience alone does not mean that we are doing what is right. God is greater than our hearts, and He knows all things. The Holy Spirit lives within us to guide us, but we must be careful not to harden our hearts against His guidance by continuing in sin. When our hearts condemn us, we can turn to God, confess our sins, and trust in the forgiveness we have through Jesus Christ.

We can always bring our needs and the needs of others before God in prayer. But does God listen? John tells us that we can have confidence that the Father hears us as we keep His commands and do what pleases Him. We also read in Proverbs 15:29:

The LORD is far from the wicked, but he hears the prayer of the righteous.

God does not approve of a life spent pursuing selfish desires while ignoring His commands. When we recognize our sin, we can turn to Him for forgiveness and ask for the strength to live differently. As we follow His commands and seek to do what pleases Him, we can come before the Father with confidence that He hears our prayers.

Jesus tells us that when we bring an offering to the altar and remember that someone has something against us, we should first go and seek reconciliation. A vengeful, hateful heart is blind and walks in darkness. This is a lesson I had to learn in life. Forgiving others and seeking peace can be of greater benefit to us than to the other person. It allows us to live our own lives instead of being held captive by what someone else has done to us.

Years can go by without those who harmed us even thinking about us, yet we continue to suffer as we replay the wrong over and over again, night after night, damaging our quality of life and our service to the Lord. The victory comes when we forgive from the heart, just as we have been forgiven in the Lord. Even when reconciliation is not possible, we can ask God to help us let go of resentment to walk freely.

God’s call to us is simple: believe in His Son, Jesus Christ, and love one another. Love does not walk in resentment and hate. Hatred can become an emotional burden that keeps us from showing love to others, especially those closest to us, as we continue to nurse a grudge.

We are free to come to God and ask for strength to overcome. If God sent His Son to die for us, how much more will He give us what we need to live for Him? God gives us grace in our times of darkness. It is God who makes us right with Him through Jesus Christ, forgives our sins, and strengthens us to do His will. We can come before Him with confidence, knowing that we belong to the truth and that He has given us His Spirit.

**Reflection Questions**

1. Have I done anything this week that my conscience and the Holy Spirit have warned me against?

2. Is there someone in my life with whom I need to seek peace or make amends?

3. Am I holding on to a past hurt, allowing resentment to continue affecting my life and my relationships with others?

**Prayer**

Heavenly Father,

I know that it is easy to hold grudges. Help me to see that these thoughts are hurting not only me but also those around me whom I love. Help me to recognize when I am allowing someone who has hurt me to continue living in my thoughts and affecting my quality of life. Strengthen me so that I might forgive others as You have forgiven me of debts often much greater than what another person might owe me. Let me walk in Your ways and enjoy fellowship with You. Please show me the things in my life that are hindering my fellowship with You in prayer, and give me the strength to turn away from them. In Jesus’ name I pray.

Amen.
