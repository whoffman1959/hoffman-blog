**DAY 86 – TREASURE OF HEAVEN**

**Matthew 13:44-46 CSB**

[45] "Again, the kingdom of heaven is like a merchant in search of fine pearls. [46] When he found one priceless pearl, he went and sold everything he had and bought it.

**No more Weeping**

As we read today's Scriptures and consider the passages surrounding them, we discover a wonderful promise of joy and eternal life. We also find a personal responsibility to be ambassadors for Christ, allowing His light to shine through us so that others might discover the same precious treasure we have found in Him.

Throughout history, many believers have given their very lives rather than turn away from Jesus. They understood that what awaited them in God's kingdom was worth more than anything this world could offer. Some suffered terribly at the hands of others, yet they held firmly to their faith. Jesus promises that the righteous will shine like the sun in their Father's kingdom (Matthew 13:43). And in Revelation, we are given a beautiful glimpse of that promised future. God will dwell with His people, wiping away every tear. There will be no more death, grief, crying, or pain. What a wonderful treasure awaits those who belong to Him!

We may never be called to suffer as those early believers did, but following Christ can still require sacrifice. When we come to believe in Jesus, it is as though the scales begin to fall from our eyes. We see things differently. The truth of His gospel becomes more precious than the possessions, recognition, and pleasures we once pursued. Things that used to glimmer in our eyes, tempting us to chase after them even when they were contrary to God's will, begin to lose their attraction. We recognize them for what they are—rust in a decaying world. Through Christ, we become new creations, learning to seek the things of God rather than the passing treasures of this life.

Jesus illustrates the value of God's kingdom through two simple pictures. One man discovers treasure hidden in a field, and another merchant finds a pearl of extraordinary value. Both recognize that what they have found is worth everything they possess. In the same way, when we truly understand the gift of salvation and the promise of eternal life through Jesus Christ, nothing this world offers can compare. We cannot purchase our salvation, for it is God's gift of grace received through faith. But discovering that gift changes what we treasure and how we choose to live.

Yet Jesus also gives us a sobering reminder in the verses that follow. He describes a large fishing net thrown into the sea, gathering fish of every kind. When the net is brought ashore, the good fish are collected, while the worthless are thrown away. Jesus explains that this points to the final judgment, when the righteous and the wicked will be separated. It is a difficult truth to consider, but one we should not ignore. Those who reject God's gift of salvation through His Son will not share in the eternal life He promises. The same Savior who speaks of the treasure of heaven also warns us of the consequences of rejecting Him.

This should stir compassion within our hearts, not a desire to condemn others. We have been offered such a wonderful gift through Jesus Christ. How could we keep that treasure to ourselves while others around us have yet to discover it?

We have a responsibility to share the love of Christ with those closest to us, with strangers, and even with those who may consider themselves our enemies. But sharing the gospel does not always mean standing on a street corner preaching to a crowd. Sometimes it begins with a simple kind word, a helping hand offered to a neighbor, or a friendly conversation over coffee. Someone may notice our faith through the way we treat others, the peace we demonstrate during difficult times, or the kindness we show when nothing is expected in return.

Some are called to preach from the rooftops, and others are called to serve as missionaries in distant lands. But every believer has opportunities to share the love of Jesus in ordinary moments. We need not force every conversation into a sermon. Sometimes the most meaningful opportunities come naturally, as we listen to a friend, share our experiences, and speak honestly about what Christ has done in our lives.

The thought that remains with me is this: We have discovered a treasure greater than anything this world can offer. How could we not want others to find it too?

One day, the troubles and sorrows of this life will pass away. For those who belong to Christ, there will be no more weeping, no more pain, and no more death. We will dwell in the presence of our Heavenly Father, enjoying the gift of eternal life through His Son. Until that day, may we treasure what He has given us and lovingly help others discover the hope we have found in Jesus.

**Reflection Questions**

1. Jesus compares the kingdom of heaven to a hidden treasure and a priceless pearl. Have there been things in your life that once seemed important but have lost their value as your relationship with Christ has grown?

2. Revelation promises a day when God will wipe away every tear and there will be no more death, grief, or pain. How does that promise encourage you when you face difficulties or sorrow in this life?

3. Sharing the love of Christ does not always require preaching to a crowd. Is there someone in your life who might be encouraged by a kind word, a thoughtful act, or a simple conversation about the hope you have in Jesus?

**Prayer**

Heavenly Father,

Thank You for the wonderful treasure You have given us through Your Son, Jesus Christ. We are grateful for the forgiveness of our sins and the promise of eternal life in Your presence. Forgive us when we become distracted by the glittering things of this world and forget what is truly important. Help us to treasure our relationship with You above everything else. Give us compassionate hearts for those who have not yet come to know Your Son, and guide us in sharing His love through our words, our actions, and the way we live each day. When trials and sorrows come, remind us of Your promise that one day there will be no more weeping, pain, or death. Until that glorious day, help us to remain faithful and to be a light for others. In Jesus' precious name we pray.

Amen.
