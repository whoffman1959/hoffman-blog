**DAY 33 – ONLY SON OF GOD**

**Scripture**

**2 Peter 1:16-21 CSB**

For we did not follow cleverly contrived myths when we made known to you the power and coming of our Lord Jesus Christ; instead, we were eyewitnesses of his majesty. [17] For he received honor and glory from God the Father when the voice came to him from the Majestic Glory, saying "This is my beloved Son, with whom I am well-pleased!" [18] We ourselves heard this voice when it came from heaven while we were with him on the holy mountain. [19] We also have the prophetic word strongly confirmed, and you will do well to pay attention to it, as to a lamp shining in a dark place, until the day dawns and the morning star rises in your hearts. [20] Above all, you know this: No prophecy of Scripture comes from the prophet's own interpretation, [21] because no prophecy ever came by the will of man; instead, men spoke from God as they were carried along by the Holy Spirit.

**John 3:16-17 CSB**

For God loved the world in this way: He gave his one and only Son, so that everyone who believes in him will not perish but have eternal life. [17] For God did not send his Son into the world to condemn the world, but to save the world through him.

**Witnesses of His Majesty**

There is a precious moment in church when we honor the reading of God’s Word from the Gospels. Everyone stands together as a community of believers as the words given by God through the Holy Spirit are read aloud. We agree together that these words are holy, special, and important for us to hear.

Peter points us to powerful testimonies that Jesus is the Son of God, the Anointed One sent by God into the world to save us. The first group of testimonies comes from those who actually saw and heard Jesus during His earthly ministry and is recorded in the New Testament. Among these are the times when our Father in Heaven spoke within the hearing of others, declaring that Jesus is indeed His beloved Son. The first was when Jesus was baptized. These accounts are found in Matthew chapter 3, Mark chapter 1, and Luke chapter 3.
The other was during the Transfiguration, when Peter, James, and John saw Jesus transfigured and speaking with Moses and Elijah on the mountain. There they heard the voice of the Father declaring that Jesus is His beloved Son. These accounts are found in Matthew chapter 17, Mark chapter 9, and Luke chapter 9.

The writers of the New Testament give us an account of what the disciples of Jesus saw and heard, but there is more testimony about Jesus for us to see in the Old Testament. The Old Testament contains hundreds of prophecies and prophetic passages that Christians understand as pointing to Jesus as the Messiah. These prophecies were given by God through the Holy Spirit and written by the prophets of old. Yet today there are many skeptics and others who will not even open the Bible to see the treasures that are within.

There are some who do, however. A number of well-known skeptics from different professional backgrounds have carefully investigated the claims of Christianity and come to believe. Among them have been a journalist and legal editor, a cold-case homicide detective, an advertising professional and writer, and a student who was preparing for a career in law. They approached the claims of Christianity with serious questions and skepticism, but their investigations led them toward faith rather than away from it. Their stories remind us that someone who opposes or doubts the truth today may yet come to believe.

There is a prayer in Ephesians chapter 1 that should rest in the hearts of all believers as we pray for those around us who do not believe:

"I pray that the God of our Lord Jesus Christ, the glorious Father, would give you the Spirit of wisdom and revelation in the knowledge of him. I pray that the eyes of your heart may be enlightened so that you may know what is the hope of his calling, what is the wealth of his glorious inheritance in the saints, and what is the immeasurable greatness of his power toward us who believe, according to the mighty working of his strength."

God’s Word is like a bright lamp shining in the darkness. We know that the testimony concerning God’s Son Jesus came through eyewitnesses who saw and heard Him during His earthly ministry and through the prophets of the Old Testament. These prophets did not speak merely from their own interpretation or will, but spoke from God as they were carried along by the Holy Spirit.

God says that His Word will not return to Him empty. Some have a Bible in the corner collecting dust. Maybe it is an older Bible passed down through generations. It could be a new Bible placed prominently in the home but seldom opened. Yet its pages contain treasures. Its words contain the message of the Light of the World. In Scripture we find the very words God has given to us. How wonderful it is to open this cherished possession and discover the treasures within.

**Reflection Questions**

1. Do I pray for those who do not believe, especially those who are close to me, that the Holy Spirit would work in their hearts so that they might believe? Have I shared the Word of God with them?

2. When I am in church, do I relish and absorb the Word of God as it is spoken aloud?
3. Do I set aside times during the week to find a quiet place, open the pages of the Bible, and read the very words God has given to us?

**Prayer**

Heavenly Father,

I pray that You would give me the desire to search Your Word for truth, especially the truth about Your Son. I pray that You would open my understanding and give me wisdom and revelation to know Your Son more and more. Let me and those around me know the hope of Your calling and the inheritance of eternal life in Your Kingdom through Your Son. Let me glimpse the immeasurable greatness of Your power toward us who believe. I pray that those who do not believe will open Your Word and see the truth written within. Let my life be so changed that people will see the results of Your Word in me as a witness to You. All these things I pray in Jesus’ name.

Amen.
