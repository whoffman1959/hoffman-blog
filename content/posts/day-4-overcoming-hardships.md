**DAY 4 – OVERCOMING HARDSHIPS**

**Scripture**

**Proverbs 10:24–25 (CSB)**

"What the wicked dreads will come to him, but what the righteous desire will be given to them. [25] When the whirlwind passes, the wicked are no more, but the righteous are secure forever."

**Matthew 7:24–27 (CSB)**

"Therefore, everyone who hears these words of mine and acts on them will be like a wise man who built his house on the rock. [25] The rain fell, the rivers rose, and the winds blew and pounded that house. Yet it didn't collapse, because its foundation was on the rock. [26] But everyone who hears these words of mine and doesn't act on them will be like a foolish man who built his house on the sand. [27] The rain fell, the rivers rose, the winds blew and pounded that house, and it collapsed. It collapsed with a great crash."

**The Father's Deliverance**

When I was a young child, until the day I joined the service, my mother was the spiritual leader in our home—as many mothers still are today. She made sure my siblings and I were in church regularly. She helped us learn Scripture, guided us even when we weren’t very lovable, and planted seeds that would take years to grow. Proverbs 22:6 gives the wisdom of teaching a child in the way they should go so that when they are older, they will not depart from it. That early foundation is what brought me back to the Father years later, after I had walked away and chased the things of the world. When I finally returned—broken, empty, and hungry for truth—the Father welcomed me back. The waters had risen, the winds had blown, and spiritually I had been washed away. Since then, the storms have been stronger, the floods deeper, yet I have stood—not because of my strength, but because my roots found their way back to the source of life, the foundation that is secure.

Many grow up in Christian homes and learn about faith from childhood; others come to Jesus later in life—wounded, weary, and searching for something real. Jesus receives them all. He promised that whoever knocks, the door will be opened. Yet some linger at the edges of faith. They attend church, know the words, enjoy the music, but never fully surrender their hearts. It’s like dipping a toe in the water without ever diving in. Others reject Him outright, raising a fist toward Heaven in anger or disbelief.

But storms come to everyone—believer and unbeliever alike. Life brings loss, pain, disappointment, and hardship. None of us escape that. The difference lies in the foundation beneath our feet. Those who build their lives on Christ may be battered, shaken, and bruised, but they are not destroyed. Their strength does not come from themselves, but from the Rock that cannot be moved.

Even if persecution comes, even if death itself stands at the door, those who belong to Christ remain secure. They stand on the unbreakable promise of eternal life—of being welcomed home by their Savior and Heavenly Father. But for those who reject Christ, there is no such anchor. When the whirlwind comes, their house cannot stand.

**Reflection Questions**

1. What is the foundation of my faith—am I standing firmly on Christ, or leaning on something that can be shaken?

2. How do I respond when life’s storms hit—do I turn to Jesus for strength, or try to endure in my own power?

3. Who in my life might be “circling faith” without ever fully surrendering, and how can I gently encourage them to trust Christ completely?

**Prayer**

Lord Jesus,

Thank You for being my Rock and my Refuge. Life’s storms will come, but I know that when my trust is in You, I will not be swept away. Forgive me for the times I’ve tried to stand on my own strength or built on foundations that crumble. Help me to build my life fully on Your Word and Your promises. Strengthen my faith, and make me a light to others who are searching for solid ground.

Amen.
