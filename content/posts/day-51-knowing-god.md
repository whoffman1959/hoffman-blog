**DAY 51 – KNOWING GOD**

**Scripture**

**1 John 4:7-15 CSB**

Dear friends, let us love one another, because love is from God, and everyone who loves has been born of God and knows God. [8] The one who does not love does not know God, because God is love. [9] God's love was revealed among us in this way: God sent his one and only Son into the world so that we might live through him. [10] Love consists in this: not that we loved God, but that he loved us and sent his Son to be the atoning sacrifice for our sins. [11] Dear friends, if God loved us in this way, we also must love one another. [12] No one has ever seen God. If we love one another, God remains in us and his love is made complete in us. [13] This is how we know that we remain in him and he in us: He has given us of his Spirit. [14] And we have seen and we testify that the Father has sent his Son as the world's Savior. [15] Whoever confesses that Jesus is the Son of God-God remains in him and he in God.

**Matthew 15:19 CSB**

For from the heart come evil thoughts, murders, adulteries, sexual immoralities, thefts, false testimonies, slander.

**God is Love**

There is a danger in this fallen world of losing sight of the truth that God is love. Sin comes from the human heart, and many people are hurt by the sin and selfishness around us. We also know that same struggle within our own hearts. Yet God did not abandon us. Out of His love, He sent His Son to give us freedom from sin and hope in this fallen world.

As God's children, our lives should reflect the love of the God we know—a sacrificial love demonstrated by sending His only Son so that we might live. When we read the prophecy of Jesus in Isaiah 53, we see the depth of the love God and His Son Jesus have for us.

Jesus had no form or majesty that people should look upon Him. He was despised and rejected by those around Him. He was a man of sorrows and acquainted with grief. Jesus cried for those who suffered and showed them love, healing them. He carried our sorrows and griefs upon Himself. He was oppressed and afflicted, but He did not open His mouth. He died so that we might live. He was silent as He went to His death, like a lamb going to the slaughter. He was taken away from this world through betrayal and the terrifying acts of men in power. He was hung outside the city that should have declared Him King. But Jesus never turned away from His love for us or from what He came to do to save us. No violence or deceit was found in Him.

Why would God allow His only Son to take our place and suffer for us so that we can live? It is because God is love, and God loves you and me. It is a personal love. We fail in so many ways to show God’s love, but God still loves us in His Son.

At different times, we all run away and fall into the same trap as Adam and Eve. We want to be our own god, lifting ourselves and our achievements up while pushing God aside. Instead of allowing God to rule in our hearts, we try to throw off what we see as His restraints and live on our own, forgetting that everything we have comes from Him, even our very existence. But God gives more grace. He comes after us in love, reveals Himself to us, and calls us back home to Him. This is love: loving rebellious children and wanting them to return.

And this same unselfish love that God shows us, we are to show to others by forgiving, living at peace with everyone if possible, building people up instead of destroying them, and caring about those around us. God changes lives. He gives His Spirit to those who follow Him, and we are to show the love of God so that others might see it and come to know the truth of His love for us. We tell others about Jesus so that they too can see the light and love of God and come to know the Creator who cares so much for us. As followers of God, we confess that Jesus Christ is the Son of God, not only through our words, but by allowing His love to be seen in our actions.

**Reflection Questions**

1. How do I view God today? Do I see a God who does not love, or a God who loves us and sent His Son to die for us?

2. Do I walk in God’s love today, showing His love through forgiving, giving, and caring for others?

3. If I do not attend a church, can I find a group of believers to fellowship with and share God’s love, both inside and outside the place where they meet? Can I show this same love to a fallen world?

**Prayer**

Heavenly Father,

Allow me to always know that You are love, that You care for Your creation and want everyone to be saved and come to the truth. Do not let the lies of the world turn me away from You, but help me to see You as You are. Let me love as Jesus does, unselfishly, putting others ahead of myself. Allow me to always remember that my very existence depends on You and that You love us so much that You sent Your Son to die for our sins. Help me to show that same love to others.
We pray this in Jesus’ name.

Amen.
