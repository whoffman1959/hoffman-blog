**DAY 56 – BORN OF GOD**

**Scripture**

**1 John 5:18-21 CSB**

We know that everyone who has been born of God does not sin, but the one who is born of God keeps him, and the evil one does not touch him. [19] We know that we are of God, and the whole world is under the sway of the evil one. [20] And we know that the Son of God has come and has given us understanding so that we may know the true one. We are in the true one-that is, in his Son, Jesus Christ. He is the true God and eternal life. [21] Little children, guard yourselves from idols.

**1 Corinthians 2:10-12 CSB**

Now God has revealed these things to us by the Spirit, since the Spirit searches everything, even the depths of God. [11] For who knows a person's thoughts except his spirit within him? In the same way, no one knows the thoughts of God except the Spirit of God. [12] Now we have not received the spirit of the world, but the Spirit who comes from God, so that we may understand what has been freely given to us by God.

**Walking in the Spirit**

What are the characteristics of those who are born of God? Galatians chapter 5 gives us a picture. Those who have the fruit of the Spirit show love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, and self-control. Those of us who belong to Christ are called to put to death the things of the flesh. These include sexual immorality, idolatry, hatred, jealousy, and the list goes on. There should be a difference between the life we once lived according to the ways of the world and the life we now live as children of God. We do not become perfect overnight, and being born of God does not mean that we never stumble into sin. It means that sin should no longer rule our lives. The direction of our lives begins to change as the Spirit works within us and teaches us to walk in the ways of our Father.

One of the iconic pictures that I love to see is that of a young child putting on their dad's shoes and walking around the house. The oversized shoes land ceremoniously on the floor as the child walks, imitating their father. This is a picture of us as Christians. We are learning to walk like our Father in Heaven and to become like His Son. We have been given the Holy Spirit, who is Himself God and knows the depths of God. The Spirit works within us, giving us understanding so that we may know what God has freely given us and learn to walk in His ways.

For some who have rejected God and lived according to the ways of the world, turning to Christ can be like stepping from darkness into a blinding light. Some describe the change as a total change of personality. The things that once controlled their lives no longer have the same power, as a new desire to do the will of God begins to grow.

But how blessed is the person who has known the Father from their youth. They may not have experienced the darkness that others have fallen into, but their experience is just as important. A life spent walking faithfully with God is also a testimony. Both lives can point to the work of the Spirit, changing us and leading us in the things of God.

When we look at the world around us, we can see what John means when he says that the whole world is under the sway of the evil one. But those who have been born of God are called to walk differently. Through faith in Christ, we overcome the world rather than allowing the world to control us. We are children of God, and our true home is not found in the things of this world. We have eternal life in His Son, are filled with God's Spirit, and are called to live as children who belong to our Father.

**Reflection Questions**

1. Do I show the characteristics of a fallen world, or the fruit of the Spirit, as I go about my life in this world?

2. What are the characteristics of those who walk by the Spirit of God? Are those characteristics becoming more evident in my life?

3. What are the characteristics of those who are controlled by the ways of this world? Are there any of these things that I need to turn away from?

**Prayer**

Heavenly Father,

Thank You for calling me to be Your child. If I have fallen short in the past, or if I am falling short today, please forgive me through the blood of Your dear Son, Jesus. Give me the strength and the ears to listen to Your Spirit as I walk through this life. Thank You for calling me out of the ways of this world. Let the love, joy, and peace of Your Spirit grow within me as I learn to walk in Your ways. Let me become more like Your Son, Jesus daily. Thank You, Jesus, for giving Yourself so that I might live. It is in Your name I pray.

Amen.
